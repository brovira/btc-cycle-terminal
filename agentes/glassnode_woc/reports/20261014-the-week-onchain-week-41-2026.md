# The Week On-Chain — Boxed In

## Executive Summary
- Buyers of the past two years hold a cost basis above **$88K** and have already turned price back twice this year.
- Bitcoin sits above the True Market Mean and has bounced twice off the US Spot ETF Cost Basis near **$81.5K**.
- Long-term holders have mostly stopped selling at a loss; holders in profit now outspend holders in loss, as in earlier recoveries.
- The late-September ETF bid faded and the funds are net sellers again.
- Liquidation clusters of similar size sit on both sides of price; the largest Binance bids have gathered at **$80K**.
- Unlike 2018 and 2022, this bear market never closed below the Realized Price, and its low stayed well above the LTH Cost Basis.
- September CPI printed hot, with an October rate decision and the US midterms ahead.
- Altcoin froth is high coin by coin but still low market-wide.

## Overhead Supply — Two Holder Groups Above Price
- Coins bought 6-12 months ago (late 2025 rally and the decline after) have a cost basis of about **$88K**; coins bought 1-2 years ago sit near **$100K**.
- Both lines have already rejected price this year: the rise to the 6-12 month basis in **January** was followed by a drop of **more than a third**; the touch of the 1-2 year basis in **May** was followed by a drop of **about a quarter**.
- 1-2 year holders also sold more into the early-October rally.
- Until a daily close above **$88K**, each advance meets holders able to exit near break-even.

## Support — True Market Mean, STH Basis and the ETF Cost Basis
- Price is above the **True Market Mean (~$78K)**, the average cost basis of active investors, and further above the STH Cost Basis; it has held above both since **September 18** (the shaded bullish regime).
- The **US Spot ETF Cost Basis (~$81.5K)** is the nearest line: Bitcoin dipped under it on **October 8** and **October 13**, but both days closed back above. Every daily close since **September 21** has held above it.
- A daily close below **$81.5K** would put the average ETF holder at a loss and remove the nearest on-chain support.

## Seller Exhaustion — Loss-Taking Fades
- LTH Realized Loss peaked on **August 12** and has dropped about **85%** since. In 2022 the peak in these losses came within weeks of the cycle low.
- Spent Output Price Distribution: in every bear market since 2011 loss-spending exceeded profit-spending, and the reverse in every bull market. At the **June** low, coins spent at a loss outnumbered those spent in profit about **12 to 1**.
- The balance flipped in **late August**; now about **4 coins are spent in profit per 1 at a loss**. Profit-taking can slow a rally, but the same ratio held in past bull markets.

## ETF Flows — The Bid Did Not Last
- Between **September 21 and October 6** the US spot ETFs took in nearly **$3B** (breakout above **$85K**), the fastest buying since **November 2025**.
- Since **October 7**: about **$760M** of net outflows.
- With ETFs selling and top buyers selling near break-even, fresh demand is thin. Steady ETF inflows returning would be the clearest sign demand is back.

## Futures Positioning — Liquidation Heatmap
- The shelf of liquidation levels around price thinned by more than **a fifth** in a week (two-month heatmap).
- The largest clusters, similar in size, sit just under **$80K** and near **$88K**.
- Below **$80K**, forced selling of longs could deepen the drop. A push into **$88K** (the 6-12 month cost basis) could force shorts to cover and lift price for a while, but without spot demand that squeeze would meet the top buyers' selling and be hard to sustain.

## Order Book — Bids at $80K
- The $81K Binance bids flagged last week were traded through on **October 8**; the largest bid block is now one level lower at **$80K**, grown sharply late on October 8 and stable since.
- It sits just beneath the ETF Cost Basis, so **$80K-$81.5K** is where order-book and on-chain support overlap.
- A break below it brings the liquidation cluster under **$80K** and then the True Market Mean near **$78K** into play.

## Cycle Structure — A Shallow Bottom
- In 2018 and 2022 Bitcoin spent months below the Realized Price. This bear market never closed below it; even the lowest close, **June 30**, was about **10% above** the Realized Price and further above the LTH Cost Basis.
- A shallow bottom means the average holder never went underwater. With price back above the True Market Mean and STH Cost Basis, the cycle looks early in a recovery.

## Macro — Hot CPI, Fed and Midterms
- September CPI (released October 14): headline **3.7%**, core **2.6%**, above forecasts. Bitcoin dipped within minutes. Household inflation expectations keep rising; their gap over core is the widest in three years.
- The Fed hiked **25 bp on September 16** (first hike since 2023). Bitcoin barely dipped over three days and was more than **10% higher** a week later.
- After the print, futures raised the odds of a second hike at the meeting ending **October 28**; US midterms on **November 3**.

## Altcoins — Altcoin Froth Indicator
- The indicator combines coin froth (how many altcoins carry heavy leverage versus their own history) and global froth (same test on the whole altcoin market), each ranked against history.
- Coin froth has been in its **top fifth** since **September 28**; global froth is low and has not been in its top fifth since the run-up to the **October 2025** crash.
- Single-coin froth with a calm aggregate implies pockets of crowded leverage, the most exposed if Bitcoin slides toward the **$80K** bids.

## Conclusion
- Bitcoin is boxed in: firm support below, heavy supply above. Near term calls for caution (ETF selling, hot CPI before the rate decision and midterms, two-year buyers waiting near break-even above **$88K**). The longer view is stronger (loss-taking dried up, profit-holders lead spending, Realized Price never lost in this bear market).
- **Bullish confirmation:** a daily close above **$88K** with ETF inflows returning = demand absorbing the overhead supply.
- **Bearish / invalidation:** a break of the **$80K** bids and the **$81.5K** ETF Cost Basis opens the way to the True Market Mean near **$78K**.
