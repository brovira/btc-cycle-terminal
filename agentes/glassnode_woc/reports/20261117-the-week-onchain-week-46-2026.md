## Executive Summary
- The crash to **$62K on Nov 15** printed classic local-bottom conditions: realized losses at capitulation scale, leverage flushed, and the biggest resting bids since August sitting below price.
- Price is defending the **$62K-$66K** block of recent-buyer cost basis, but broad demand remains thin. A **daily close below the block** could trigger a fast drop toward the **Realized Price at $53.7K**.
- Side theme (sponsored): perpetuals on real-world assets (RWA perps) went from about **190** listings at the start of the year to more than **3,200**; open interest is about **$15B**.

## Capitulation: Realized Loss
- Realized Loss (loss locked in when coins move below their purchase price) compared with the past year's realized profits has hit a level last seen at the lows of **2018, 2020, 2022 and early 2023**. Such spikes have marked capitulation.
- Sellers were mostly recent buyers: the 1-3 year cohort has been quiet since taking its last big profits in late 2025, while coins under 6 months old took heavy losses into early 2026, banked gains in the Sep-Oct rally, and are selling at a loss again.
- Spot is below the average cost of both groups. While that persists, further loss-selling from recent buyers is plausible.

## The Support Block: STH Cost Basis Distribution
- The block around the August low, **$62K-$66K** (flagged as the only dense support in last week's edition), holds roughly **one fifth** of short-term-holder coins. It grew by about **66K BTC** over the two heaviest selling days (dip-buying).
- Short-term holders bought very few coins below **$62K**, and the largest remaining long-liquidation cluster sits just under it.
- **Invalidation / bear path:** a daily close below the block puts August buyers underwater and forces out leveraged longs together, which could turn a break into a fast drop. Targets: the **June low at $58.5K**, then the **Realized Price at $53.7K** (average price at which all coins last moved).
- **Upside marker:** the **Short-Term Holder Cost Basis at $74.1K** is the aggregate break-even of recent buyers. A reclaim would confirm a bottom is forming.

## Leverage Flush: Liquidations
- Nov 13-14 saw some of the largest long liquidations relative to open interest since **2023**. Most longs stacked above **$62K** are gone; last week's thin gap between price and the **~$60K** cluster was run through.
- The remaining cluster spans **$59.5K-$62.2K**. The Nov 15 low clipped its upper edge without reaching the core. A revisit could bring a final flush, though most forced selling above $62K has already occurred.
- The largest short cluster sits far above, near **$87.5K**.

## Order Book and Flows
- Within **5%** of price, resting bids on Binance and Coinbase outweigh asks by about **2.3x** (previously only slightly since late August). The brightest band near **$60K** is the largest single Binance bid level since August.
- Takers sold into those bids during the crash but have turned net buyers over the past day. Resting orders can be withdrawn, so bids matter mainly once filled.
- **Coinbase Premium:** negative almost every hour since late October, turned positive over the low's weekend (peak evening of Nov 15), and has since slipped back to a small discount. A premium that turns positive and holds would signal real US demand returning.

## Demand Is Still Thin
- **Stablecoin supply** is down **4.2%** over 90 days. Since 2022 it has tended to shrink when T-bill yields rise; the 3-month yield is up about **45 bps** in 90 days. In 2022, at the fastest yield rise, supply fell by almost a fifth in three months. If yields stop rising, the drain should ease.
- **Miners:** MARA and Riot reported sales of about **28K BTC** this month (funding pivots to AI/HPC data centers), while Strategy kept buying. Other treasury companies were net buyers in November.
- **US spot ETFs:** **$2.6B** net outflows in the ten trading days to Nov 13.

## Options and Event Calendar
- The Deribit-implied range widens steadily into 2027 with its centre near spot. For the **September 2027** expiry, options price about a **one-third** chance of settling below the Realized Price (**$53.7K**) and about **20%** of settling above the September high.
- Catalysts: NVIDIA earnings (Nov 17, after its CEO resigned last week); Anthropic's expected Nasdaq debut **Nov 19**; Fed decision **Dec 9**, a day before the November CPI print.

## Altcoin Washout
- Only **6%** of the top 100 altcoins trade above their 50-day average. The share above the 200-day average fell from over a third to **21%** in a week.
- In every year since 2023, the deepest washout fell within **4 days** of a Bitcoin local low, and Bitcoin was higher **60 days** later each time. Caveat: the 200-day share has not yet reached past lows' floors. If the summer low holds as the cycle low, precedent suggests alt selling is near exhaustion.

## Tokenization
- Live tokenized products (stocks, funds, other RWAs) rose from **428** at the start of the year to about **6,200** in early November, while Bitcoin fell **15%** over the same span. Tokenized stocks drive most of the growth, and trading is following onto exchanges.

## Sponsored Section (Gate partnership; treat claims with caution)
- RWA perps trading on tracked exchanges: from about **190** to over **3,200**. Gate ranks second by listings (fifth in January; first June-September): **490** now vs **20** at the start of the year.
- RWA perp open interest: from about **$0.35B** to about **$15B** across eight measurable exchanges. Gate holds about **$2.1B** (over 20x its January level) and has ranked third behind Binance and Hyperliquid in every weekly reading since late July.
- After last month's hack (described as the largest in crypto history), Gate published a Proof of Security; it states cold-storage/independent-custody assets would cover **104.58%** of user assets even if all hot wallets were lost. These are Gate's own figures; a third-party audit is under way. Gate Guard is a free plan covering eligible account-takeover losses up to **$200K** per event.

## Conclusion
- Bitcoin has completed a washout: capitulation-level realized losses, flushed leverage, and absorption at the August block, with takers turning to buying and a brief Coinbase premium. Broad demand is still absent (shrinking stablecoins, miner selling, ETF outflows).
- **Base case to watch:** hold of **$62K-$66K**; reclaim of the **STH Cost Basis at $74.1K** confirms a forming bottom.
- **Invalidation:** daily close below **$62K**, with few buyers and a big long-liquidation cluster beneath, opens **$58.5K** and then **$53.7K**.
