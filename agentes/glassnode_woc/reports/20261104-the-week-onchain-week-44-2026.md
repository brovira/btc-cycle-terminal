## Executive Summary
- Short-term holders went from almost all in profit in early October to about **38%** in profit; the market is far less stretched than at the September high.
- Bitcoin is retesting the levels it reclaimed in August: **True Market Mean $77.7K**, **Short-Term Holder Cost Basis $75.7K**.
- The pullback flushed much of the long leverage built since August; shorts have grown above price, largest cluster near **$87K**.
- Options lean to calls; the largest December call positions sit between **$80K and $120K**.
- Spot volume holds at about **1.5x** its pre-pump level, but US spot ETFs saw nearly **$1B** of net outflows in two weeks.
- Bitcoin wins about half of sessions vs the S&P 500, a rate not exceeded since March 2025.
- The largest hack in crypto history cost only about a **3%** dip.
- Democrats won both chambers in the midterms; this time most of the drop came before the vote.
- Altcoin short-term holders now mostly sell at a loss, versus a profit in ~**80%** of tokens in late September.

## STH Supply in Profit — Excess Worked Off
STH = coins bought within the past **155 days**. In early October STH Supply in Profit was at or above **97.5%** (the report's "sell line"). After a ~**10%** pullback from the September high it is about **38%**, so most STH coins sit at a loss. Condition: a recovery back **above 50%** with price holding would signal newer buyers back in profit and regaining confidence.

## Retest of the August Breakout
Between August and September price reclaimed levels lost after the 2025 all-time high: the 200-day average, the STH Cost Basis and the True Market Mean. Last week price held above the True Market Mean through both October sweeps. On Nov 3 it closed just below it (**$77.7K**) and dipped to **$76.1K** overnight before recovering above. Such retests are normal after a breakout. The STH Cost Basis (**$75.7K**) and the 50-week average sit just below. Condition: while price holds above them, the August structure stays intact.

## Liquidation Heatmap
- The slide ran through many long-liquidation levels built since August, lowering cascade risk.
- Remaining below price: a band at **$73K–$76K** (aligned with the STH Cost Basis and 200-day average) and a large block at **$60K–$63K** left by the August breakout.
- Above price, liquidation levels grew over the past week while price fell ~**3.5%**: traders added shorts into the decline. Largest cluster near **$87K**, close to the September high; a turn higher could force short covering and fuel a move toward it.

## Options Positioning
- Largest expiries: **Nov 27** with **$5.7B** OI and **Dec 25** with **$12.5B** OI. Max pain **$80K** and **$76K** respectively, either side of spot, so no clear directional pull.
- Across expiries: about **$18B** calls vs **$13B** puts. Largest December calls at **$80K, $90K, $100K, $120K**; largest puts at **$60K** and below (deep-fall protection). Read: upside positioning into year-end.
- Caveat: OI does not show buyer vs seller; part of the call OI is likely covered-call selling for premium, so not every call is a bullish bet.

## Volume and ETF Flows
- 7-day average total spot+ETF volume is about **1.5x** its mid-July to mid-August level and stayed above it through the pullback. Still low by ETF-era standards, but suggests the interest drawn by the August move has not left.
- US spot ETFs: nearly **$1B** net outflows over two weeks, including **$640M** on Nov 2. ETF trading ~**$1.3B**/day since May, about a third of its earlier pace.
- Read: a market driven by capital already in crypto, little new traditional money, little froth. ETF flows tend to follow price rather than lead, so their absence is a weak warning.

## Bitcoin vs Traditional Assets
Over two months: Bitcoin about **-2%**, S&P 500 less than **-1%**, European stocks slightly behind Bitcoin, gold about **-9%**. Bitcoin gave back its early-October lead but did not fall behind. Over the last **90** sessions it beat the S&P 500 in half of them (**50%** win rate), with a slightly higher average gain per session; not above 50% since March 2025. Condition: a win rate climbing above 50% and holding would show renewed relative strength.

## The Binance Hack
On Oct 29 Binance reported a hack affecting **$6.3B** in assets, the largest in crypto history (larger than the next nine combined). Bitcoin dipped ~**3%** and is back near the pre-outflow price. The muted reaction signals strength, though it does not offset the lack of outside demand seen in ETF data; if that demand returns, the market has room to move fast.

## US Midterms
Democrats won the House and Senate on Nov 3, stronger than expected. After each of the past three midterms Bitcoin made one more drop before its cycle low, **15%–50%** within **2–10 weeks**. This time Bitcoin fell ~**11%** from the September high into election day and is up ~**1.5%** since. Longer term the industry must adapt to a Democratic Congress, but Bitcoin is far more embedded in traditional finance, and three elections are a small sample.

## Altcoins
STH-SOPR above 1 = selling at profit, below 1 = at loss. In late September STH sold at a profit in ~**80%** of 300+ tokens tracked; by end-October only ~**12%**, with the typical token's STH selling at a small loss. Profit taking has turned into loss taking. Condition: if Bitcoin holds its retest, an altcoin market cleared of profit takers is a better base than September's crowded one.

## Conclusion
Bitcoin has worked off September's excess: STH mostly at a loss, altcoin holders selling at a loss, price retesting the August breakout. Volume held and the record hack barely moved price, but new money from outside crypto is still missing. Risk/reward is better than at the September high.
- **Level to watch: $73K–$76K** (STH Cost Basis + 200-day average + long-liquidation cluster).
- **Base case:** if it holds, the retest is a healthy reset within the August uptrend.
- **Invalidation:** a sustained break below **$73K** points to a longer consolidation.
