## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

- **STH Cost Basis + bandas σ (el framework central de todo el lote).** "Represents the average acquisition price of newer market participants... historically served as a key threshold delineating between bullish and bearish local regimes." Ejemplo 2025-07-29: STH CB=$105,4k, +1σ=$125,1k, +2σ=$141,6k, -1σ=$92,1k; precio sobre STH CB=señal alcista, resistencia repetida en +1σ, techo mayor en +2σ. [week-30, week-31, week-32, week-33, week-34, week-42, week-46]
- **Supply Quantile Cost Basis (0,75/0,85/0,95).** "Mapping the 0.95, 0.85 and 0.75 quantiles, indicating levels where 5%, 15% and 25% of the supply is held at a loss." Rotura del 0,95 cierra fase eufórica; caer bajo 0,85 "has often preceded deeper corrections toward the 0.75 quantile." [week-35, week-41, week-42]
- **Cost Basis Distribution (CBD) Heatmap / URPD.** Mapea dónde se concentra el cost basis del supply — clusters densos = soporte/resistencia natural. "Each band of colour reflects dense regions of cost basis, which often act as natural support or resistance zones." Se usa para identificar "air gaps" (zonas de baja liquidez de supply) que actúan como imán de precio o zonas de movimiento rápido. [week-30, week-31, week-34, week-36, week-40, week-44, week-45]
- **Cohort cost basis (1m/3m/6m holders).** "When the market trades below these levels, it often signals that newer holders are slipping into unrealized loss... a state that can provoke impatience and selling." 6-month cost basis como umbral de "fear" que acelera downside. [week-34, week-36]
- **Active Investors' Realized Price / True Market Mean.** Modelos de cost basis excluyendo coins dormidas/perdidas. Usados como soporte estructural mayor en fases correctivas: "next significant support lies around the Active Investors' Realized Price... a metric that reflects the cost basis of actively circulating supply and often marks deeper corrective phases in prior cycles." [week-43, week-44, week-46, week-47]
- **BlackRock/ETF Deposit Cost Basis vs True Market Mean/Active Investor Price.** Confluencia entre cost basis del mayor ETF y modelos de valoración on-chain como "psychological and statistical valuation anchors." [week-27]
- **Realized Profit/Loss Ratio (y su umbral).** Ratio >elevado (ej. 39,8, luego 571x) = "highly elevated... surpassed by only 1,5% of trading days" señal de profit-taking extremo; ratio colapsando a 0,07x o LTH ratio hacia 10x = señal de "liquidity has evaporated" hacia bear profundo. [week-28, week-30, week-47]
- **SOPR / Change-adjusted SOPR umbral 0,98.** "Historically, cyclical lows have only been confirmed when this metric fell below 0.98, marking widespread capitulation." [week-34]
- **STH-NUPL (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss).** Mide stress relativo al market cap; "deep negative values have coincided with capitulation phases that precede market bottom." Rangos citados: -0,05 (leve) vs -0,1/-0,2 (correcciones mid-bull) vs <-0,2 (bear profundo). [week-43]
- **Relative Unrealized Loss (umbral 5% / 10%).** "As long as unrealized losses stay within this range [<5%], the market can be classified as a mild bear phase... a deeper drawdown pushing this ratio above 10% would likely trigger broader capitulation." [week-34, week-44]
- **Trend/Accumulation Trend Score (por cohortes de wallet).** Balance entre acumulación y distribución por tamaño de wallet, usado para leer "underlying investor behaviour" sin sesgo direccional de precio. [week-27, week-40]
- **Drawdown histórico comparativo (%).** Enmarca la severidad de una corrección comparando con "mid-cycle bears" (>25%) y "deep cyclical lows" (>75%). [week-34, week-38]
- **Estructura de opciones — max pain, strike distribution, dealer gamma** (citado solo cuando cruza con dirección/nivel, p.ej. max pain $110k como "gravitational pull into expiry"; niveles de liquidación en Hyperliquid como proxy de "liquidation walls"). [week-37, week-38, week-46, week-47]
