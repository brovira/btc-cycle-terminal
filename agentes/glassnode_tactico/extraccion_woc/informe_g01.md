## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

- **SOPR** (Spent Output Profit Ratio): "a SOPR value of greater than 1 in a bear market precipitates a sharp downward price correction, while a value of less than 1 in a bull market triggers an upward correction" — umbral = 1.0 como pivote régimen. En bull, SOPR debe "resetear a cero" (retest de 1.0) antes de que termine una corrección: "SOPR needs to reset to zero before a correction is complete" (semana 49) · [glassnotes-issue-1, week-49-2020]
- **MVRV / MVRV Z-Score**: fair value = realized cap; "when the metric is too high (red-zone)... market tops, while a lower ratio (green-zone) indicates market bottoms". Umbral de suelo: Z-Score <0 (cruzado en marzo-2020, primera vez desde bear de 2018/19) · [27-feb, 2-apr-2020]
- **STH-MVRV** (Short-Term Holder MVRV): techo/suelo de ciclo corto. "when this metric has emerged from below 1 and reached 1.25, this has marked the start of bull markets... As long as STH-MVRV stays above 1... the bull market has historically tended to stay in effect" (semana 32) · [10-aug-2020]
- **NUPL** (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss): zonas de sentimiento (Fear/Capitulation/Hope/Optimism/Euphoria implícitas). "NUPL has stayed in the red zone for at least a few months before recovering back to levels of hope and then optimism" · [12-mar, 2-apr, 9-apr, 16-apr-2020]
- **STH-NUPL**: usado para timing de techos de mercado en verde y de bull markets sostenidos en no-rojo: "Short-Term Holder NUPL has been extremely accurate in timing market tops (green zone). Also, consistently staying above the red zone has indicated bull markets" · [16-apr-2020]
- **Reserve Risk** (precio / HODL Bank): "when confidence is high and price is low, there is an attractive risk/reward to invest (Reserve Risk is low)". Usado repetidamente como gauge de entrada durante todo el bull run 2020 · [5-mar, 20-jul, 8-dic-2020]
- **Percent Supply/UTXOs in Profit**: umbral 90%+ = "clearly marked pronounced bull markets"; umbral 95% = históricamente "BTC sees significant gains over a short period of time - usually around 2-3 months" · [2-jun, 17-ago-2020]
- **Realized Cap**: valora BTC al precio de último movimiento; "a consistently high (and growing) Realized Cap means that the price of BTC has more room to grow while still being in the realm of fair value" · [22-jun-2020]
- **UTXO Realized Price Distribution**: mapa de "estantes" de coste — usado para ver cuántos UTXOs están marginalmente en beneficio vs cómodamente en beneficio (creados entre $6000-$10000 vs entre $10k y precio actual) — soporte implícito en esos rangos de coste base · [3-ago-2020]
- **Relative Unrealized Profit** (normalizado por market cap): techo global histórico ~0.8; techo local 2019 en $13k = 0.64. Usado como "techo relativo" para estimar cuánto recorrido queda: en $15k valía solo 0.56 → "implying more room for BTC to grow before we see the next top" · [9-nov-2020]
- **Glassnode Compass / GNI (Glassnode Network Index)**: eje X = quarterly price trend, eje Y = GNI (fundamentals on-chain). Cuatro regímenes: Regime 1 (bullish, GNI alto + precio positivo), Regime 2/3 (transición, divergencia entre precio y fundamentals), Regime 4 (bearish, ambos cayendo). Usado semanalmente como marco de régimen macro · [todas las Week On-Chain desde semana 19]
- **Coin Days Destroyed (CDD) / ASOL**: aumento = coins viejos moviéndose = LTH tomando beneficio; usado como señal de presión de venta que puede frenar el precio a corto pero "historically, CDD increases during bull runs, but usually peaks before the top" (no señal de techo por sí sola) · [30-nov, 8-dic-2020]
- **Hodler Net Position Change**: por encima de cero = hodlers acumulando (bullish); usado explícitamente para marcar suelos ("hodlers stopped accumulating more BTC" en $10k feb-2020 = posible techo; "hodlers began accumulating" durante crash de marzo = señal de suelo) · [19-mar, 7-may-2020]
- **Stablecoin Supply Ratio (SSR)**: ratio supply BTC/stablecoins; SSR bajo = mucha "dry powder" lista para entrar en BTC = bullish, con salvedad de que ese dinero también podría ir a altcoins · [13-jul-2020]

## E) GUARDARRAÍLES

- "unprecedented global events make it more risky to predict price movements based on historical trends" (marzo-2020, sobre las métricas de suelo) · [2-abr-2020]
- "the way the market and miners respond to this adjustment may not follow historical trends" (sobre difficulty adjustment) · [26-mar-2020]
- "it took several months before BTC turned bullish after the last halving, and over a year for the price to increase by an order of magnitude" (matiz sobre expectativas del halving) · [25-may-2020]
- Correlación con mercados tradicionales citada repetidamente como el mayor riesgo que puede invalidar la lectura on-chain: "a crash in traditional markets... could halt this bull run in its tracks, as we saw on Black Thursday" · [3-ago-2020]; y "BTC's price... seems tied to the rest of the world's financial markets" (varias semanas de septiembre-octubre)
- Sobre ASOL: metodología requiere filtrar ruido — el pico de marzo-2020 resultó ser "dust" agregado por un exchange en transacciones Omni, no señal real de LTH saliendo · [5-mar-2020]
- Explícito sobre incertidumbre de interpretación en divergencias: "Could this decrease in on-chain activity signify that we have reached a local top? Or is this just a brief dip... Market observers should continue monitoring" (no da respuesta cerrada) · [20-feb-2020]
- Sobre CDD/ASOL alto en nov-2020: reconocen que patrón difiere de 2017 (el pico ocurre mientras el precio SIGUE subiendo, no tras el techo) — advierten no extrapolar directamente el patrón histórico · [30-nov-2020]
