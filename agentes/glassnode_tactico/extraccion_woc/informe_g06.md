## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

- **True Market Mean Price (Cointime Economics, con ARK Invest):** cost basis "ideal" del inversor activo. Descrito como "psychological resistance" cuando el precio está por debajo, y como techo/suelo psicológico en el URPD. Ejemplos numéricos: $29.6k (sep-23, resistencia), $29.78k (roto oct-23, "puts the average active BTC investor back into profit"), $31.0k (dic-23, "average holders recover"), $33.3k (ene-24, soporte de respaldo tras perder STH cost basis). *"The True Market Mean Price is very much a 'centroid' of the Bitcoin market, often distinguishing bull markets, from bear markets"* — `2024-01-30-...week-05-2024.md`.
- **Realized Price:** cost basis medio de todo el supply (incluye monedas antiguas/perdidas). Suelo histórico; cruzarlo pone "the average unit of BTC into profit". `$20.3k` en sep-23. `2023-09-11...` y `2023-12-05-...week-49-2023.md`.
- **STH Cost Basis (STH-MVRV=1):** nivel de "decisión" del mercado — el retest como soporte es típico en uptrend, pero *"a decisive loss of this level would bring the True Market Mean into consideration"* (jerarquía de niveles: STH cost basis → True Market Mean). $27.8k (oct-23), $28k (oct-23, roto), $38.3k (ene-24). `2023-10-17-...week-42-2023.md`, `2024-01-30-...week-05-2024.md`.
- **URPD (UTXO Realized Price Distribution):** identifica clusters de cost basis (soportes/resistencias por volumen transado) y "airgaps" (zonas con poco volumen transado = zonas de movimiento rápido). Ejemplo nov-23: cluster denso $26k-$31k (Section A), airgap $31k-$33k (Section B), cluster reciente $33k-$35k (Section C), cluster 2021-22 $35k-$40k (Section D, ~620k BTC). Conclusión táctica: *"the $30k to $31k range is a key area of interest, being the upper level of the largest supply and cost basis cluster... makes the market reaction in the event price returns to $30k of interest"* — `2023-11-07-...week-45-2023.md`.
- **LTH-MVRV + SBI (Spending Binary Indicator) — compás de 4 regímenes** (framework más explícito del lote): usa la posición del precio relativa al cost basis de LTH más si LTH está vendiendo activamente:
  - Capitulation: LTH-MVRV < 1 y SBI > 0.55
  - Transition: 1.0 < LTH-MVRV < 1.5 y SBI > 0.55
  - Equilibrium: 1.5 < LTH-MVRV < 3.5 y SBI > 0.55
  - Euphoria: LTH-MVRV > 3.5 y SBI > 0.55 (LTH con +250% profit medio, motiva venta acelerada)
  `2023-11-20-...week-47-2023.md`.
- **Active Investor Realized Price ("Fair Value", Cointime Economics):** modelo ponderado por tightness del supply. Uso: *"the path to a new ATH has always involved major fluctuations of ±50% around the Active Investor Realized Price"* — banda de ±50% como rango operativo de "fair value". ~$36k en dic-23. `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`.
- **Mayer Multiple (precio / 200D-MA):** techo técnico histórico >2.4 (sobrecomprado), suelo <0.8 (sobrevendido); ~1.5 descrito como resistencia recurrente en ciclos previos (incl. ATH nov-2021). Valor citado 1.47 (dic-23). `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`.
- **NVT Price / NVT Premium:** valoración fundamental por throughput de red (28D vs 90D). *"faster 28-day variant trading above the 90-day model"* = señal de transición bear→bull. NVT Premium con picos agudos = "potential near-term 'overvaluation' signal". `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`.
- **STH Supply Profit/Loss Ratio:** >20 = sobrecalentado; <0.05 = sobrevendido; ~1.0 = punto de equilibrio que *"tends to align with support/resistance levels within the prevailing market trend"*. En uptrend, retests de 1.0 = soporte ("buy-the-dip"); un "break to resistance" en ese nivel señala riesgo alto. `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`, `2024-02-13-...week-07-2024.md`.
- **% Supply in Profit — 4 fases de ciclo:** Bottom Discovery (<58%, -1SD), Bear/Bull Transition (58%-90%), Euphoria (>90%, +1SD). Aplicado también año-a-año: Bear Floor/Recovery, Early Bull, Euphoric Late Bull, Primary Bear. `2023-11-20-...week-47-2023.md`, `2023-12-19-...week-51-2023.md`.
- **STH Supply in Loss — umbral operativo de riesgo:** "Typical Correction" cuando STH Supply in Loss aumenta pero no supera ~60% de su holding; "High Risk" cuando supera esa super-mayoría ("top-heavy market"). `2024-02-13-...week-07-2024.md`.
- **Realized Cap sensitivity ($ necesario para mover $1 de Market Cap):** en bull tardío >$0.75-$1.0 (insostenible históricamente); en bear $0.10-$0.30; mediana histórica $0.25. Usado como medida de tightness/liquidez de mercado. `2023-11-13-...week-46-2023.md`.
- **Sell-Side Risk Ratio (STH y LTH):** valores altos = ventas con gran magnitud de profit/loss relativo al Realized Cap; valores bajos = "exhaustion" de profit/loss al precio actual. `2023-11-07-...week-45-2023.md`.
- **Investor Confidence in Trend (holders vs spenders cost basis):** Negative sentiment cuando cost basis de quien vende < cost basis de quien retiene; Positive cuando es mayor. Rango normalizado -0.25 a 0.25, zona neutral -0.05/0.05 (descrita como "arbitraria"). Intervalos de sentimiento negativo duran típicamente 1.5-3.5 meses. `2023-09-18-...week-38-2023.md`.
- **NUPL Ratio (LTH-NUPL menos STH-NUPL):** diferencia como gauge de presión financiera relativa. Entrada en rango -0.25 comparada explícitamente con 2016/2019 (fase de recuperación de bear). `2023-10-17-...week-42-2023.md`.
- **Realized HODL Ratio (RHODL) con mediana 2 años móvil:** umbral para transición régimen bull/bear. `2023-09-18-...week-38-2023.md`.

## C) REGLAS EXPLÍCITAS "cuando X → Y"

- *"When more than 97.5% of the supply acquired by new investors are at loss, the chance of seller exhaustion increases exponentially. On the other hand, when more than 97.5% of short-term holder supply is in profit, these players tend to take advantage of the opportunity and exit at break-even or with a profit."* — `2023-09-18-...week-38-2023.md`
- *"Should the trend start rising back towards the transitional and then positive territory (> -0.05), it would suggest that capital is starting to flow back into the market."* (Investor Confidence in Trend) — `2023-09-18-...week-38-2023.md`
- Modelo Risk-On/Risk-Off: **Risk-on** = BTC + ETH + Stablecoins con inflows netos simultáneos; **Risk-off** = cualquiera de los tres con outflow neto; **"Peak Altseason Mania"** = Risk-on + cambio 30D del Realized Cap de ETH y del supply de stablecoins positivo Y creciente (2ª derivada positiva). — `2023-10-10-...week-41-2023.md`
- *"When STH Supply in Profit trades above 2M BTC (90-day floor), it historically coincides with rallies towards new ATHs."* — `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`
- Regímenes LTH-MVRV/SBI (ver B) — cada régimen es explícitamente condicional (`Condiciones: LTH-MVRV < 1 and SBI > 0.55`, etc.) — `2023-11-20-...week-47-2023.md`
- *"Mayer Multiple values exceeding 2.4 [overbought], or below 0.8 [oversold]."* — `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`
- *"Profit/Loss Ratios > 20 historically align with overheating conditions. Profit/Loss Ratios < 0.05 historically align with oversold conditions. Profit/Loss Ratios ~ 1.0 signifies break-even and tends to align with support/resistance."* — `2023-12-12-...week-50-2023.md`
- *"Typical Correction Conditions — When STH Supply in Loss increases, but does not exceed ~60% of their holdings... High Risk Conditions — When STH Supply in Loss increases dramatically, often to a super-majority."* — `2024-02-13-...week-07-2024.md`
- *"A decisive loss of this level [STH Cost Basis] would bring the True Market Mean into consideration."* (jerarquía condicional de niveles) — `2024-01-30-...week-05-2024.md`
- Composite momentum index: *"flag when 4-of-8 conditions are met [uptrend sostenible]... flag when all eight conditions are met [momentum positivo extremadamente fuerte]... a composite index value of less than 4 denotes... overall negative momentum, typically experienced throughout bear markets."* — `2024-02-05-...week-06-2024.md`
- AVIV cross: *"Positive Momentum: AVIV crosses above its 365D-SMA... Negative Momentum: AVIV crosses below its 365D-SMA."* — `2024-02-05-...week-06-2024.md`
- *"A very simple momentum indicator can be achieved by tracking periods where STH-MVRV trades above or below 1.0."* — `2024-02-05-...week-06-2024.md`
- *"When this indicator [% supply in profit] trades above this upper band [+1SD ≈ 90%], it has historically aligned with the market entering the early stages of a bull market's 'Euphoric phase'."* — `2023-11-20-...week-47-2023.md`

## E) GUARDARRAÍLES (limitaciones declaradas por Glassnode)

- Sobre el modelo Risk-On/Altseason: *"What we are looking for is less the absolute magnitude (as there will be error bars around these assumptions), but more-so any significant regime shifts."* — `2023-09-11-...week-37-2023.md`
- Sobre la zona neutral del Investor Confidence in Trend: rango -0.05 a 0.05 se marca explícitamente como *"(which is arbitrary)"*. — `2023-09-18-...week-38-2023.md`
- Sobre datos de exchange balances (disclaimer repetido en casi todos los artículos desde nov-2023): *"these figures might not always encapsulate the entirety of an exchange's reserves, particularly when exchanges refrain from disclosing their official addresses. We urge users to exercise caution and discretion... Glassnode shall not be held responsible for any discrepancies or potential inaccuracies."*
- Sobre Active Entities como proxy de adopción: caída a mínimos de ciclo pese a rally de precio se explica como artefacto de Inscripciones/reuso de direcciones, no señal real — *"largely a result of the uptick in activity related to Ordinals and Inscriptions, where many participants are re-using Bitcoin addresses"* — `2024-01-30-...week-05-2024.md`, referencia cruzada a `WoC-28-2023`.
- Sobre el URPD post-Ordinals: los análisis de fees/volumen deben tener en cuenta que hay una "percepción de valor" superior al BTC realmente transferido en inscripciones — riesgo de sobreestimar actividad monetaria real. — `2023-09-26-...week-39-2023.md` (aunque este artículo queda fuera del scope táctico, el guardarraíl es reutilizable).
- Sobre el Composite Momentum Index: se describe como ponderación *"evenly weighted"* simple, sin justificar por qué 4-de-8 es el umbral salvo por observación histórica — no se declara backtesting riguroso. — `2024-02-05-...week-06-2024.md`
- Todos los artículos cierran con disclaimer estándar: *"This report does not provide any investment advice... No investment decision shall be based on the information provided here."*
- Sobre el disclaimer explícito de comparación entre ciclos: reconocen reiteradamente que las comparaciones (2015-17, 2018-22, 2019-20, etc.) son "similares" o "eerily similar" pero nunca afirman repetición garantizada — uso consistente de lenguaje probabilístico ("suggests", "would suggest", "historically aligned").
