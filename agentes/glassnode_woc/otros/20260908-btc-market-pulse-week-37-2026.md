## Executive Summary
- Bitcoin is range-bound near **$79,100**, up **0.7%** on the week, trading inside a **$77,300–$81,300** band.
- Spot momentum cooled hard — down **30%** to **54.6** — while spot selling pressure is fading rather than reversing into buying.
- Leverage is expanding (futures and options open interest both above their upper statistical bands) even as funding costs shrink, and options positioning leans bullish.
- ETF inflows have re-accelerated sharply and ETF holders are back in aggregate profit.
- On-chain transactional activity is soft, but capital inflow metrics and supply profitability are both running hot.

## Spot Market — Cooling Momentum, Fading Sell Pressure
Price sits at **$79,100**, a **0.7%** weekly gain confined to a **$77,300–$81,300** range as the late-August rally stalls into consolidation. Spot momentum fell **30%** to **54.6**, back to the middle of its statistical bands after last week's stretched reading. Spot volume was roughly flat at **$5.3B**. The spot cumulative volume delta narrowed from **-$84.9M** to **-$29.6M** — sellers are losing conviction, but there is no sign yet of a buyer-driven reversal.

## Derivatives — Leverage Builds, Options Lean Bullish
Futures open interest rose **1.0%** to **$37.1B**, above its upper statistical band, while long-side funding payments fell **32.8%** to **$1.3M** — leverage is growing without a matching aggressive bid for upside. Perpetual CVD recovered from **-$423.2M** to **-$62.1M**.

Options carry the clearest directional signal: open interest climbed **2.1%** to **$40.1B**, also above its upper band; the volatility spread widened to **-20.9%** (implied vol trading below realized vol); and the 25-delta skew flipped from **0.79%** to **-2.05%**, both readings below their lower bands — indicating unusually strong call demand relative to puts.

## Institutional Demand — ETF Flows Re-Accelerate
US spot ETFs took in **$681.2M** of net inflows, up from **$247.8M** the prior period, even as ETF trade volume fell **19.2%** to **$12.1B**. ETF MVRV swung from **-0.54** to **1.31**, above its upper band, putting ETF holders back in aggregate profit.

## On-Chain Activity — Quiet
Entity-adjusted transfer volume fell **12.8%** to **$5.0B**, fee volume dropped **4.8%** to **$213.0K**, and active addresses held flat at **635.6K** — all readings inside their normal statistical bands, signaling subdued on-chain transactional activity relative to the derivatives and ETF picture.

## Capital Flows & Profitability — The Strongest Signal
Monthly realized cap change accelerated to **0.8%** and hot capital share climbed to **30.1%**, both well above their upper bands — price-sensitive capital continues entering the network. Profitability is elevated across the supply: **69.3%** of coins are in profit, NUPL sits far above its upper band, and the realized profit-to-loss ratio is back at parity at **1.0**.

## Takeaway
Price is flat while leverage, fresh capital inflows and profit realization all build underneath — derivatives and ETF positioning point toward upside exposure even though spot momentum has cooled and on-chain transactional activity stays subdued.
