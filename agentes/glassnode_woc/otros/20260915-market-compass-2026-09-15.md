## Executive Summary
- The Composite fell to **23/100 (Defensive)**, two points below a week ago and five below a month ago.
- The drop is almost entirely explained by a single lens: Macro dropped **8 points** on the week to **31/100**, crossing back into Restrictive.
- On-Chain Fundamentals was the only lens to improve, rising to **52**, the best reading of this run.
- Capital Flows, Cycle Position and Investor Behaviour all held unchanged.
- The broad cross-asset rally from late August has already faded, with the median 7-day return across assets back near zero.
- Bitcoin holders used the bounce to sell: the Realized Profit/Loss Ratio set a new run high of **2.63**.

## Composite — One Lens Moved the Whole Board
The composite score fell three points to **23/100 (Defensive)**. The deterioration is not evenly spread: Macro lost **8 points on the week** (down to **31/100**) and crossed back into **Restrictive**, giving back three weeks of macro repair in a single session. Against that, On-Chain Fundamentals rose to **52**, its best reading of the current stretch, while Capital Flows, Cycle Position and Investor Behaviour stayed exactly where they were a week earlier. The trigger identified in the article: the dollar index returned to its 200-day average.

## Late-August Breadth — Already Faded
In late August the entire field of assets ran up together — the largest burst of cross-asset gains this year — with the Altcoin Season Index back at **75** and mid caps leading the thirty-day table. The article frames this kind of broad, simultaneous rally as historically associated with risk zones rather than the start of a durable advance. That read has already played out: the median seven-day return across assets is back near zero, and the index has already touched and lost the altseason line once.

## Bitcoin — The Bounce Got Sold
Bitcoin bounced to trade near a **47% premium to its realized price**. The holders behind that bounce used it to sell: realized flow flipped from mostly loss to mostly profit over the past three weeks, split about evenly between long- and short-term holders, while the loss side of the flow thinned to a fraction of the total. The Realized Profit/Loss Ratio set another run high at **2.63**. The article notes this one-sided profit-taking pattern matches what showed up through the 2024 and 2025 advances, and that the cycle lens's expected roll-off has not started yet.

## What Flips the Read
- **Bearish for the Composite:** the dollar closing above its 200-day average would confirm Macro's reversal and push the composite lower.
- **Bullish offset:** Investor Behaviour finally turning up would offset that deterioration.
