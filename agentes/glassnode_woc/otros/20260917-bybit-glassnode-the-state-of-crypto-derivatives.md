## Core thesis
The crypto derivatives landscape has changed shape structurally: options moved from a marginal product to a very significant share of notional open interest on crypto-native venues, while dated futures (fixed-expiry futures) have effectively disappeared as a relevant category.

## Stated numeric data
- Options went from being "a sideshow" to accounting for **nearly half (~50%) of notional open interest** on crypto-native venues.
- Dated futures **have all but disappeared** from relevant activity (no explicit percentage given in the available text).

## Structure of the analysis
The joint report examines the shift in three passes:
1. **The whole market** — the aggregate picture of the mix shift between options, perpetuals, and dated futures.
2. **The venues within it** — how that activity is distributed across the different exchanges/platforms.
3. **The conditions the new structure is pricing** — what market information (volatility, risk, sentiment) is now embedded in that new product mix.

Every chart in the report draws on Glassnode's derivatives and market data across the exchanges it tracks.
