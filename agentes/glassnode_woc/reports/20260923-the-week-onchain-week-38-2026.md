## On-chain — The Bear Market That Never Broke the Floor
Unlike the 2018-2019 and 2022-2023 downturns, where price traded below the Realized Price for months, this cycle's June low stayed above it — the first bear-market low since 2017 to do so. At that low, the share of coins held at a loss (inverse of % Supply in Profit) matched the November 2022 trough, but the magnitude of those losses was far smaller: NUPL, which nets total unrealized profit against loss across the supply, never went negative this cycle, versus deep negative readings in 2018 and 2022. Smaller aggregate losses historically mean less urgency to sell. Price has since moved back above both the **True Market Mean (~$77K)** and the Short-Term Holder Cost Basis, after briefly testing the Mean from below last week. **If price holds above the True Market Mean, the June low stands as the shallowest bear-market low on record since 2017.**

## On-chain — Long-Term Holder Supply and the Mean MVRV Ceiling
The market now trades just under the densest block of long-term-holder supply, concentrated at **$84K-$85K**, and above the call-option cluster that had previously capped price. The next structural resistance above that is the mean MVRV price at **$96.7K** — the Realized Price scaled by Bitcoin's long-run average MVRV multiple, marking the level where the average holder's unrealized profit returns to its historical norm. Buyers who entered one-to-two years ago, near the top of that cohort's range, also reach break-even close to this level. **Holding above $84K keeps the path to $96.7K open; a fall back below $84K would put the $77K True Market Mean back in play as support.**

## Off-chain — Options Dealers Position for $95K-$97K
Deribit gamma positioning shifted sharply in a single session: negative gamma built up in the **spot-to-$92K** zone, where dealer hedging tends to buy rallies and sell dips (amplifying moves), while positive gamma around the **$95K** strikes jumped to its highest reading on record, where hedging instead dampens price swings. Because $95K sits just under the $96.7K mean-MVRV resistance, the **$95K-$97K** band is framed as the first major test of the advance if it continues.

## On-chain — Profit-Taking Still Light
Weekly Net Realized Profit/Loss during the current rally is running at only a fraction of the levels seen at the 2024 and 2025 tops — a pace closer to the early leg of the late-2023-to-early-2024 uptrend than to a mature, selling-heavy rally. **A rise in weekly realized profit toward 2024/2025-top magnitudes would signal holders distributing into strength; sustained low profit-taking argues the rally still has room.**

## On-chain — Short-Term Holders Near the Sell Line
Nearly all short-term holders are now in profit, and their Supply in Profit share has crossed above the historical "sell line" that has preceded increased selling in the past — early in recoveries (2019, 2023) as well as near tops (2021, 2025), so the signal alone is not directional. Realized profit across all cohorts remains low, meaning the incentive to sell has not yet converted into actual heavy selling. **A drop back below the sell line combined with rising realized profit would be the first concrete warning that recent buyers are taking profit.**

## Off-chain — ETF Demand Returns
US spot Bitcoin ETFs absorbed roughly **$1.3B in net inflows over the five trading days** since the current squeeze began, following two straight weeks of outflows; the most recent day was the largest single-day inflow since the start of July. **Continued inflows near this pace would keep ETF demand as a support for the advance.**

## Market Structure — Spot Volume Recovery and Exchange Rotation
24h spot volume across covered exchanges has more than doubled off its August low, up **121%** since the rally started. The context matters: from late 2025 through mid-2026, every prior spot-volume expansion (four in a row) occurred on falling price — capitulatory selling. This is the first expansion in about a year to coincide with rising price instead. Still, the 7-day average volume sits roughly **30% below** where it stood a year ago, so this is a recovery off the floor rather than a return to 2025-era turnover. **A sustained hold above the pre-rally volume range would confirm a durable bid rather than a temporary squeeze.**

Beneath the top of the exchange league table, share has been rotating: Gate has risen 4 places over the past two years to rank #3 by BTC spot volume, holding a top-three spot in 9 of the last 24 months and growing its share of covered volume from 2.0% to 9.1% (+7.1 points, the largest gain of any venue). Poloniex gained 3 places and Bybit 1, while four venues lost ground. Binance has remained #1 in every one of the last 24 months, still clearing roughly **31%** of covered spot volume. Overall, 9 venues gained share and 3 lost it over the two-year window — broad-based competition rather than one challenger, meaning the returning flow is spreading across many order books.

## Off-chain — Altcoins Rally, Leverage Stays Flat
Over the past week, **72.5%** of tracked altcoins outperformed Bitcoin, well above the **39%** peak share seen during the August squeeze. Yet altcoin perpetual futures open interest (in coin terms) has barely grown over 30 days, with fewer than half of tracked markets adding positions — a sharp contrast with the overheated leverage build-ups of February 2021 and December 2024, when the same measure rose sharply with most markets participating. The move looks spot-driven, which lowers the odds of a forced-selling cascade. **A broad jump in altcoin open interest would be the signal that the rally is starting to overheat.**

## Conclusion
Bitcoin has cleared the True Market Mean and the long-term-holder supply block that capped price for most of 2026, and the June low held above the Realized Price — the shallowest bear-market low since 2017 as long as **$77K** support holds. Profit-taking remains light, ETF buying is returning, and altcoins are rallying without a meaningful leverage build. The next test sits at **$95K-$97K**, where options gamma positioning and the **$96.7K** mean-MVRV level converge. **Holding above $84K keeps that path open; a break back below $84K, followed by a break below $77K, would put the recovery in doubt.**
