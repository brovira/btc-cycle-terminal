## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

1. **UTXO Realised Price Distribution (URPD)** — perfil de volumen por precio de coste. "Zones where significant clusters of volume arise... represents a concentration of cost basis." Ejemplos: $31.0k-$34.3k = 10.5% supply, soporte fuerte, "few comparatively few on-chain support levels below until $18.8k" `[W29]`; tres bandas: $31-40k floor (2.98M BTC), $45-50k rango actual (1.65M BTC, "podría actuar como soporte fuerte"), $53.7-59k resistencia potencial (1.336M BTC en pérdida) `[W35]`; $36.2k-$41.2k = 12.41% supply `[W6-2022]`.

2. **Realised Price (coste base agregado)** — "generally provides reliable price support and bear market floors". Se calcula por cohortes: agregado, STH, LTH. Ejemplo `[W1-2022]`: STH $51.4k (resistencia si el precio la reconquista), agregado $24.4k, LTH $17.7k ("visual observation of LTHs valuing Bitcoin at a higher floor value").

3. **MVRV Ratio (mkt cap / realised cap)** — usado para identificar régimen: "3x Early-Bull, 2x Mid-Bear, 1x Double Pump" `[W30]`. Como Z-Score, <0.85 marca tres sub-casos: corrección en bull (rebote a nuevos máximos), bear temprano tras blow-off (capitulación + rebote fuerte), o bear medio/tardío (pérdidas sostenidas) `[W4-2022]`.

4. **STH-MVRV** — nivel 1.0 actúa como resistencia psicológica ("getting their money back"); históricamente pocos periodos cortos con STH-MVRV<1 `[W1-2022, W4-2022]`.

5. **NUPL (Net Unrealised Profit/Loss)** — 0.5 marca punto de inflexión histórico entre "Bear Market Relief Rally" (2014,2018,fin mini-bull 2019) y "Bull Market Disbelief Rally" `[W33]`.

6. **LTH/STH Supply Ratio** — supply squeezes mayores han ocurrido cuando STH Supply Ratio llega a 20% `[W32]`; umbrales históricos de LTH supply que preceden squeezes: 65% (2x en 2013), 75% (2017), 80% (2020) `[W29]`.

7. **Mayer Multiple (precio/200DMA)** — <0.8 = descuento >20%, correlaciona con drawdowns severos y capitulaciones (ene-2015, nov-2018, mar-2020); da nivel de precio exacto: "$39.1k which the market has now reclaimed (calculated as 0.8 x 200DMA)" `[W6-2022]`.

8. **Realised-to-Liveliness Ratio (RTLR) / "HODLer Fair Value"** — amplifica el realised price cuando la Liveliness cae (más dormancia). "RTLR will indicate a higher fair value... when HODLers are accumulating"; converge al Realised Price cuando se distribuye. Da niveles: $39.2k `[W4-2022]`, reconquista de $39,958 `[W6-2022]`; y en sentido inverso, STH Realised Price por encima del Liveliness Price señala que "the bearish trend has some time left in it to reestablish a price floor" `[W8-2022]`.

9. **Thermocap Multiples (2x, 6x, 32x, 64x)** — "Each cycle adds to the minimum monetary premium at the end of bear markets. The raw Thermocap (1x) caught the 2011 bear bottom, 2x Thermocap caught the 2015 market, and 6x Thermocap provided support for 2018-19 and March 2020. Bull cycle tops have generally peaked between 32x and 64x." Da niveles numéricos: 64x=$1.94T ($103k BTC), 32x=$969B `[W38-2021]`.

10. **LTH-SOPR como coste base implícito** — "A LTH-SOPR value of 2.0 at a BTC price of $36k gives an aggregate LTH cost basis of $36k/2.0 = $18k". Usado para inferir rango de coste base de venta: lower bound SOPR=1.5→$24-31k, upper bound SOPR=2.0→$18-23k `[W38-2021]`; y el modelo "LTH Spent Price" derivado como price/LTH-SOPR, comparado contra Realised Price y Liveliness Price como punto medio de "falta de dirección macro" `[W8-2022]`.

11. **STH-SOPR** — resistencia/soporte en 1.0: "SOPR resets to 1 and then bounces higher... signals a disbelief"; ruptura sostenida bajo 1.0 = "bearish relief" `[W31, W32, W1-2022, W4-2022, W6-2022]`.

12. **% Supply/Transfer Volume in Profit** — umbrales explícitos: >65% = spending bullish (fortaleza), <40% = capitulación histórica `[W3-2022]`.

13. **HODL waves / Spent Output Age Bands** — banda joven (<1 mes o <3 meses) que se hincha = distribución de monedas viejas entrando en circulación (bajista); banda joven que se contrae = maduración/acumulación (alcista). Usado repetidamente en casi todos los artículos como framework de "invalidación" del caso alcista/bajista.

## C) REGLAS EXPLÍCITAS "CUANDO X → Y"

- "**A: SOPR breaks above 1.0**... **B: SOPR reaches a local high**... **C: SOPR resets back to 1.0**... Most important to watch is whether SOPR holds above 1.0. Should SOPR continue to trade higher, this reflects a bullish scenario... If on the other hand, SOPR falls and trades back below 1.0 on a sustained basis, it would suggest a general weakness" `[2021-08-09-week-32]`

- "**aSOPR resets to 1 and then bounces higher**: This indicates... (**Disbelief**). **aSOPR falls back below 1 and stays there**: This indicates... (**Bearish Relief**)" `[2021-08-02-week-31]`

- "**30D Crossing Below 60D** is generally a signal of **income stress**... **30D Crossing Above 60D** is generally a sign of **hash-rate recovery** and miner capitulation" (Hash-Ribbons) `[2021-08-16-week-33]`

- "**Rapid hash-rate recovery** may suggest offline miners have successfully relocated... reducing the risk of treasury liquidation sell-pressure. **Slow hash-rate recovery** may suggest the converse... increases the risk of treasury sales" `[2021-07-12-week-28, repetido 2021-07-05]`

- "Major Supply Squeezes have historically occurred when the STH Supply Ratio hits 20%" `[2021-08-09-week-32]`

- "**Percent of Transfer Volume in Profit > 65%** signals that a large amount of coins are being spent in profit... **< 40%** signals... this historically occurs in market downtrends and especially capitulation events" `[2022-01-17-week-03]`

- "**Positive values (MRG)** signal that a bull trend is in tact... **Negative values** signal that a bear trend is in play... **Large values** signal that Bitcoin is possibly overbought (positive) or oversold (negative)" `[2022-01-17-week-03]`

- "**Negative Values (red, LTH-STH RealCap delta)** signal that the STH Realized Cap is increasing more... occurs during bull runs... **Positive Values (green)** signal that the LTH Realized Cap is increasing more... occurs during bearish accumulation markets" `[2022-01-17-week-03]`

- "It is favourable for price if young coin supply continues to decline... Conversely, large spikes in young coin supply suggests renewed distribution and would favour a bearish bias" `[2021-07-12-week-28]`

- "Periods where Futures Open Interest exceeds >= 2% of Market Cap are short-lived, and tend to end with a dramatic flush of margin" `[2022-01-10-week-02]`

- "**High Puell Multiples** indicate strong profitability and thus high incentives for miners to sell... **Low Puell Multiples** indicate poor profitability, miner capitulation... eventually leading to a supply squeeze" `[2021-07-05-week-27]` — con guardarraíl inmediato de que la caída de esa semana fue un artefacto técnico, no señal real (ver sección E).

## E) GUARDARRAÍLES (limitaciones que ellos mismos declaran)

- **Puell Multiple distorsionado por ajuste de dificultad:** "The principle reason the Puell Multiple dropped like a stone... is actually a technical one: Block-times slowed down by 226%... only 40% of the expected BTC was issued... The difficulty adjustment has since corrected this." Aviso explícito de no leer la señal en bruto sin contexto `[2021-07-05-week-27]`.

- **Los eventos raros no se cumplen "limpiamente":** "If the current rate of coin maturation (14.75k BTC/day) persists, LTHs would hold 80%... in around 2-months (although this is not likely to play quite so cleanly, since we know many coins from Mar-May were spent and sold)" `[2021-07-19-week-29]`.

- **Standard vs entity-adjusted Liveliness pueden divergir y llevar a lecturas erróneas:** "the standard liveliness metric jumped significantly higher... However, the entity adjusted version does not see the same event, which indicates these coins are classified as 'internal'" `[2021-08-02-week-31]`.

- **Outliers de una sola wallet distorsionan métricas de lifespan/volumen:** el gasto de 94,643 BTC del hackeo de Bitfinex 2016 disparó CDD y Liveliness — "As noted above... One lifespan metric that can be deployed to cut through anomalies such as this one... is ASOL", porque ASOL ignora volumen y no fue afectada — reconocen que ciertas métricas (CDD, Dormancy, Liveliness) son vulnerables a eventos anómalos de gran volumen y otras (ASOL) no `[2022-02-07-week-06]`.

- **El STH Supply Ratio de 20% "often holding that level for some time"** — el umbral no es un cruce instantáneo y limpio, matiza la interpretación de la señal de squeeze `[2021-08-09-week-32]`.

- **De-leveraging puede ocurrir sin cruzar el umbral de 2%:** "De-leveraging events can occur in either direction, and at times have triggered despite an Open Interest leverage ratio below 2%, like on September 7 when El Salvador made Bitcoin legal tender" — reconocen que el umbral no es determinista `[2022-01-10-week-02]`.

- **El análisis de "whale holdings" requiere matiz:** "it is important to note that interpretation of 'whale holdings' requires a very nuanced analysis approach, and is rarely as straight forward as watching changes in an aggregate wallet cohort" `[2022-02-14-week-07]`.

- **Bear markets son difíciles de definir con métricas simples:** "Bear markets are hard to define in Bitcoin, as the traditional 20% drawdown metric would trigger a bear almost every second Tuesday given the volatility. Thus we look to investor psychology and profitability as a gauge" `[2022-01-24-week-04]` — guardarraíl metodológico explícito sobre por qué usan perfiles on-chain en vez de drawdown técnico.

- **Incertidumbre binaria reconocida explícitamente al cierre de varios artículos:** "It remains to be seen whether the market can turn the macro trend around... If so, it would resemble the 2013 'double pump' market. If not, the probability of being a mid-Bear fractal may increase" `[2021-07-26-week-30]` — no se comprometen a una sola lectura cuando la evidencia es mixta.
