# The Week On-Chain — Quiet Under $85K

## Executive Summary
- Most of the **$653M** US spot ETF inflow of Oct 19 has left again (**$540M** out on Oct 22); net intake over two weeks is only **$67M**. ETF volume has stayed low since May.
- Long-term holders carry a heavy block of coins bought above **$85K**; the nearest sizeable bids on Binance have shifted down to **$78K-79K**.
- The **$84K-85K** band has rejected price four times this year, and Bitcoin sits just below the US Spot ETF Cost Basis (**$81.5K**).
- Macro is soft ahead of the Oct 28 Fed decision: the Leading Economic Index has stalled and Consumer Confidence is near its 2009 low.
- The 2026 rebound tracked 2023 for ~3 months and then lagged, though more wallet cohorts are accumulating than in summer 2023.
- Volatility is back at summer-2023 levels; calmer than this on only ~5% of days since 2015.
- Each dip since August has realized smaller losses; entity-adjusted profit taking is light.
- Hyperliquid HIP-3 (stocks/indices/commodities) volume again exceeds its BTC perpetual volume; altcoin breadth has peaked and is falling.

## ETF Flows — Demand Fading
Last week's **$653M** inflow (Oct 19) has mostly reversed, including **$540M** out on Oct 22; net of the past two weeks: **+$67M**. Since early May the funds have traded about **$1.3B/day**, and on Oct 23 volume dropped to ~**$700M**, one of the quietest sessions of the year. Read: ETFs add little demand at current prices. Confirmation of renewed demand: inflows returning on rising volume.

## Cost Basis Distribution — Overhead and Support
- **Long-term holders** (>155 days): about **4M BTC** bought between **$85K and $118K**, mostly in 2025; over half of their coins were bought above current price. A recovery toward **$85K** may trigger break-even selling from them.
- **Short-term holders**: most coins were bought below spot. Densest zone near **$77K**, a second cluster near **$64K** (2026 lows). These newer buyers are the likeliest to defend dips.

## Order Book — Bids Migrate Lower
Binance spot bids at **$80K** refilled after the Oct 15 sweep but were removed again by the Oct 24 sweep and have not returned (only **$3.4M** left at $80K). The largest nearby block is now at **$78K-79K**, about **$42M** (up from ~$24M in mid-October): the first place buyers are expected on a dip.

## The $84K-85K Band
- About **4.3%** of all BTC last moved in this band, mostly on a single day (Nov 22, 2025) via one large holder's internal transfer; price has nonetheless respected it.
- Support in December (bounces Dec 1 and Dec 18); broke Jan 31 after a failed push to $84K, followed by a ~**20%** drop.
- Resistance since then: May, early September, Oct 21. September high **$87.1K** (Sep 23). Only **5** daily closes above the band since January, never more than 3 consecutive.
- **Invalidation/flip condition:** a run of daily closes above **$85K** turns the band back into support.

## Cost Basis Ladder
- **US Spot ETF Cost Basis: $81.5K** (price just below it). A daily close back above it puts the average ETF holder in profit.
- **True Market Mean: $77.9K** — held through both October sweeps.
- **Short-Term Holder Cost Basis: $75.8K** — next level below.

## Macro — Soft Backdrop Into the Fed
The Leading Economic Index's September reading equalled July's (no further improvement). Consumer Confidence fell **7.1 points** over two months to **48.1**, near its lowest since 2009. The Fed hiked **25 bp** on Sep 16 (first hike since 2023). Another hike on Oct 28 would tighten conditions for risk assets as growth stalls; the 2023 analog also occurred under a hiking Fed (last hike July 2023).

## 2023 Analog and Accumulation
- From each cycle low, 2026 followed 2023 for ~3 months; both had a short squeeze around day **50**. Since then 2026 lags: **+38%** above the June low versus **+75%** at the same stage in 2023.
- Accumulation Trend Score: **100-1K BTC** wallets are the steadiest buyers and still adding; **10-100 BTC** and **>10K BTC** added most of the month but slowed recently; other cohorts are reducing. Better than summer 2023 (all cohorts reducing). More cohorts joining the 100-1K group would signal broader demand.

## Volatility
One-month Realized Volatility is ~**25%**, below the past-year range and similar to summer 2023 (low of **18%** in August 2023). Calmer on only ~**5%** of days since 2015. Setting resembles 2023 (calm after a rebound and altcoin froth). If compression persists into the Oct 28 Fed decision, the decision could trigger the next move.

## Realized Profit and Loss
- August low: daily realized losses exceeded profits by ~**$630M**; around the Oct 24 sweep the largest daily net loss was only ~**$27M** — fewer holders sell at a loss on dips.
- Oct 22 spike (standard measure: Realized Profit ~**$3.7B**, Realized Loss ~**$1.4B**) is an internal transfer by one entity, not selling. Entity-adjusted profit taking: ~**$260M/day** this week, about half the pace of the September-high week.
- Larger losses on the next dip would indicate sellers returning.

## Hyperliquid — Speculation in Traditional Assets
HIP-3 markets traded ~**$2.6B/day** versus ~**$2.4B/day** for Bitcoin perpetuals. In early August HIP-3 volume exceeded 3x BTC's; it fell to about half by late August and is now back above. Top markets: SK Hynix, S&P 500, a tech index, then crude oil. Continued HIP-3 growth with flat BTC perp volume = speculative money still favoring stocks.

## Altcoin Breadth
Share of top-100 altcoins above their 200-day average peaked at **88%** on Oct 4 (above the early-2023 high of **80%**), now **66%**; for the top 500, **42%**. In 2023 breadth peaked in February, bounced in April, faded through summer. If breadth keeps falling while BTC holds its range, risk appetite is fading in altcoins first.

## Conclusion — Scenarios and Levels
Range-bound, quiet market: thin demand (ETF flows netted out, few cohorts adding) but light selling (smaller dip losses).
- **Support:** bids **$78K-79K**; True Market Mean **$77.9K**; then STH Cost Basis **$75.8K** (next support if a daily close breaks below the True Market Mean).
- **Resistance:** **$81.5K** ETF Cost Basis, **$84K-85K** band.
- **Upside condition:** daily closes back above **$85K** are the first step; last week's fuller confirmation was a daily close above **$87.5K** with ETF inflows repeating on rising volume (demand absorbing overhead supply).
- **Downside condition:** daily close below **$77.9K**.
- **Catalyst:** Fed decision on Oct 28, with compressed volatility.

*Data as of Oct 27, 2026 (price, order book, hourly on-chain) and Oct 25-26 (daily on-chain, ETF flows, Hyperliquid); macro series show September releases. Recent points subject to revision.*
