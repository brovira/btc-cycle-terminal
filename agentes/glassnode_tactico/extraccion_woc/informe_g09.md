## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

1. **STH Cost Basis ± 1σ (bandas)** — el más usado. "The Short-Term Holder cost basis has historically acted as an important reference level across bull-market uptrends... typically acted as a sort of upper and lower bound for local price action" [WoC09]. Ejemplo WoC09/14: +1σ=$130k/$131k, CB=$92k/$93k, -1σ=$71k/$72k. WoC21 amplía con bandas +0,5σ y +1σ ($120,3k/$135,7k) como techo local: "price has traded between these two bands for 467 days, and above the +1σ level for 484 days... just 17.5% of Bitcoin's trading history" — entrada infrecuente, tope de acción local.

2. **Active Realized Price / True Market Mean** — "with 50% of trading days above and below the Active Realized Price, one can consider this level as a key threshold separating a bull and a bear market" [WoC09]. WoC14: AR Price $71k, True Market Mean $65k — "confluence... perhaps being the final defence line for the bulls". Aplicado a ETH en WoC20 ($2.400/$2.900).

3. **CBD Heatmap / URPD (mapa de calor de coste base)** — visualiza dónde se concentró oferta por precio, identificando "air gaps" (baja densidad = soporte débil, movimientos rápidos) vs clusters densos (soporte/resistencia real). WoC08: air gap $70k-$88k. WoC10: acumulación $95k-$98k. WoC19/25: zona $93k-$95k / $93k-$100k como soporte estructural mientras el precio la sostenga. WoC22: "areas that once supported the rally have flipped into sources of resistance, as earlier holders take the opportunity to offload" — zonas históricas de acumulación pueden invertirse en distribución cuando el precio las revisita desde arriba.

4. **MVRV Ratio con bandas ±1σ sobre su media histórica** — framework explícito de régimen [WoC17]: "Below -1 Standard Deviation: Deep bear market. Between -1 and long-term mean: Recovery phase. Above long-term mean: Early bull market. Above +1 Standard Deviation: Euphoric bull market." Ejemplo: media 1,74; Realized Price +0,5σ $100,2k, +1σ $119,4k [WoC21].

5. **% Supply in Profit vs media de largo plazo** — "Bull markets are typically characterized with the supply in profit above its long-term mean, routinely finding support at this level... Bear markets are historically punctuated with deep and sustained periods below the long-term mean" [WoC15].

6. **Sell-Side Risk Ratio** — "High values indicate...market likely needs to re-find equilibrium and usually follows a high volatility price move. Low values indicate...a degree of equilibrium has been reached...usually describes a low volatility environment" [WoC17, repetido en WoC20 y WoC26].

7. **Realized Supply Density (concentración ±10% del spot)** — a mayor densidad, mayor sensibilidad y potencial de volatilidad ante pequeños movimientos [WoC09, WoC18, WoC23].

8. **Spent Supply Distribution (SSD) Quantiles** — niveles escalonados por percentil del coste base de la oferta gastada [WoC22]: "absolute euphoria tends to occur when price trades above the 0.95 quantile, while sideways bull phases often unfold between 0.85 and 0.95... sustained trading below the 0.75 quantile has typically marked bearish or risk-off periods." Ejemplo: q0,95=$103,7k, q0,85=$95,6k.

9. **Umbral de gasto de LTH ≈ +350% de ganancia no realizada** — "Historically speaking, the Long-Term Holder cohort typically ramps up their spending pressure when the average member is holding a +350% unrealized profit margin" [WoC17]; reconciliado a nivel de precio: $99,9k.

10. **Medias móviles técnicas (111DMA, 200DMA, 365DMA) en confluencia con niveles on-chain** — usadas repetidamente junto al STH-CB para marcar zonas de "debe romperse y sostenerse" [WoC14, WoC17, WoC21, WoC23].

11. **"Ribbon" de coste base por sub-cohortes STH** (<24h, <1sem, <1m, <3m, <6m) — "a sort of fast-to-slow ribbon of cost basis levels, providing a form of momentum indicator" [WoC12, WoC17, WoC23].

12. **Unrealized Loss per Percent Drawdown** — normaliza la pérdida no realizada STH por la profundidad del drawdown para comparar severidad entre correcciones [WoC15].

13. **Supply-in-Loss Realized Price / Supply-in-Loss MVRV** — coste base y MVRV calculados solo sobre monedas bajo el agua, oscilador para medir profundidad del estrés vs ciclos previos [WoC13].

## C) REGLAS EXPLÍCITAS "cuando X → Y"

- "When this ratio falls below 1.0, it signals that more capital is being locked in a loss rather than a profit. These retests of the 1.0 level often coincide with local capitulation events and short-term price reversals" [WoC13, Realized P/L Ratio].
- "Death Cross" on-chain: cruce del precio VWAP 1-mes bajo el de 6-meses — "This pattern has historically preceded 3–6 month-long bearish trends" [WoC13].
- Framework de régimen MVRV citado en B4 [WoC17].
- "Bull markets are typically characterized with the supply in profit above its long-term mean... Bear markets are historically punctuated with deep and sustained periods below the long-term mean, with frequent rejections at this level" [WoC15].
- Sell-Side Risk Ratio, framework citado en B6 [WoC17, WoC20, WoC26].
- "So long as this ratio [STH Supply P/L Ratio] remains well above the equilibrium level of 1.0, bullish momentum tends to stay intact. However, any sustained drop below this level would signal a meaningful shift in market strength and possible trend exhaustion" [WoC19].
- Umbral de gasto LTH +350%, citado en B9 [WoC17].
- SSD Quantiles, framework citado en B8 [WoC22].
- SSR: "Low SSR signals greater stablecoin-denominated buying power relative to BTC supply. High SSR suggests reduced stablecoin purchasing capacity relative to BTC, potentially reflecting weaker demand" [WoC26].
- Exchange Buying Power: "Positive values and uptrends indicate...greater stablecoin buying power...may support increased demand and liquidity... Negative values and downtrends reflect...weaker demand and tighter liquidity" [WoC26].
- "These are levels that must be broken and held for further price appreciation, as a rejection of this level would push the price back into bearish territory" [WoC17, sobre 111DMA/STH-CB].
- "STH-SOPR trading consistently above 1.0 is a characteristic of bullish uptrends" [WoC16].
- "Swift and short undercuts at the equilibrium level of 1.0 [SOPR] suggests that investors are buying and defending their cost-basis, which is a typical characteristic of bull markets" [WoC09, repetido WoC17].
- "Historically, a typical euphoria phase tends to follow this metric [% Supply in Profit] stabilizing above 90% for an extended period" [WoC16].

## E) GUARDARRAÍLES (limitaciones declaradas por Glassnode)

- WoC08: "a continued lack of demand could extend the current downtrend" — condicionan la señal de agotamiento vendedor a que aparezca demanda real.
- WoC13: cuestionan su propia lectura — "Are these rebounds signs of a larger bullish impulse or simply reactive bounces within a broader downtrend?" y concluyen "there is little evidence suggesting a structural bullish shift in momentum is underway."
- WoC15: sobre LTH envejeciendo hacia pérdida — "an important nuance is emerging... As of now, there is no clear evidence to suggest such a regime shift is underway" (reconocen que la señal de confirmación de mercado bajista vía LTH suele llegar con retraso respecto al techo).
- WoC15 (Realized Cap held in loss): aclaran que comparar en términos absolutos entre ciclos engaña — "Whilst the Realized Cap held in loss is larger (given Bitcoin is a larger asset today), individual investors are less challenged today compared to prior bear market periods."
- WoC16: "much like the July–September 2024 period, this move has thus far only resulted in a momentary reclaim of the STH-Cost Basis... has not yet confirmed a complete regime shift back into bullish territory" — advierten contra sobreinterpretar una recuperación puntual de nivel.
- WoC18: "very low IV regimes tend to precede a period of heightened realized volatility, serving as a valuable counter-indicator" — su propia lectura de baja IV puede ser señal contraria.
- WoC19: sobre el skew de opciones — "While not a definitive signal on its own, a sustained negative skew... often aligns with rising market optimism" (matizan que no es señal suficiente por sí sola).
- WoC21: guardarraíl explícito sobre el Accumulation Trend Score — "it is important to recognize that overwhelming consensus behavior is not always a reliable indicator of future direction (and can in fact be a contrarian one)", ilustrado con el ATH de nov-2021 donde acumulación fuerte precedió al techo cíclico.
- WoC22: hedgean su lectura de zonas CBD como soporte — "These zones now potentially can serve as supply-dense regions that may act as short-term support, depending on broader market sentiment."
