# The Week On-Chain — Strength Meets a Wall

**Fuente:** Glassnode Research · *The Week On-Chain* (Week 39, 2026) — https://research.glassnode.com/the-week-onchain-week-39-2026/
**Autor:** Glassnode · **Fecha:** 2026-09-30
**Tipo:** newsletter semanal (texto). Cita como `[reports/20260930-the-week-onchain-week-39-2026.md]`.

---

## Executive Summary
- US spot ETF demand spiked to roughly **$1B/day on Sep 21 and Sep 22** (the first was the biggest day in almost a year), then faded; price has gone sideways since.
- Profit taking remains light compared with past tops, but long-term holders are now the main sellers.
- Bitcoin has beaten the S&P 500 in more than half of the last 30 sessions, yet it lost more than stocks this week.
- A large Binance spot ask wall at **$85K-$85.5K** caps price; the **True Market Mean at $77.2K** is the first support below.
- Total volume (~**$6.4B/day**) is near the bottom of its post-ETF-launch range: the rally is early and speculative.
- Altcoins beat Bitcoin over 30 days without froth or new leverage, but momentum stalled this week.

## ETF Flows
- US Spot ETF Net Flows peaked at about **$1B on each of Sep 21 and Sep 22**.
- Inflows have shrunk every trading day since, down to **$24M on Sep 28** (latest data point); price moved sideways.
- Funds are still net buyers, but at a small fraction of the peak pace.
- Confirmation: a return to inflows near the **~$1B/day** pace would be the clearest renewed-demand signal.

## Profit Taking
- Weekly Net Realized Profit/Loss runs well below the average week seen at the 2024 and 2025 tops; its pace now resembles the start of the late-2023 uptrend.
- The mix has shifted: long-term holders (coins held **>155 days**) nearly doubled realized profit in the week to Sep 29 versus the breakout week, and their share of total realized profit rose from **34% to 55%**.
- Warning condition: weekly profit taking climbing toward past-top levels would mean holders are selling into strength.

## Bitcoin vs. the S&P 500
- Over the last 30 sessions BTC has reacted less to S&P down days than to up days; this pattern has held since **Aug 20**, though the window still contains August's rally.
- This week the edge slipped: **Sep 22 to Sep 29 BTC -2.9% vs S&P -1.2%**, and BTC fell harder than the index on the index's down days.
- Invalidation: if the favourable stretch flips to red as August sessions roll out of the window, the edge over equities is gone.
- BTC outperformed the S&P in more than half of the last 30 sessions; the share crossed **50% on Sep 24**, the first time since mid-May.
- The margin is thin and a few losing sessions would push it below half. Staying above 50% into October would show a durable edge rather than a short streak.

## Price Levels and Cost Basis
- Prior report named **$84K** as the level to hold; price has slipped just below it.
- Binance spot book wall at **$85K-$85.5K**: it appeared on Sep 24 and has **tripled** in size. Price has entered its lower half but not broken through.
- While the orders stay, this wall is the key level; a sustained break above **$85.5K** would clear the largest resistance on the book.
- **Short-Term Holder Cost Basis (coins <155 days): $73.3K**, price about **13%** above it, so recent buyers are in profit. In the 2023 and 2024 uptrends price stayed above this line for months. A fall below would return recent buyers to a loss, as in most of H1 2026.
- **True Market Mean: $77.2K**, about **8%** below price; price has held above it since **Sep 18**.
- In 2023-2025, stretches above both the True Market Mean and the STH Cost Basis marked the stronger uptrend phases.
- First support to watch: a **daily close below $77.2K** would end the current stretch.

## Volume
- Prior report noted spot volume had more than doubled from its August low, but from a very low base.
- Total BTC volume (spot exchanges + US spot ETFs) averages about **$6.4B/day**, near the bottom of its range since the ETFs launched.
- With such thin volume the rally is early and speculative, without broad demand.
- Confirmation: a sustained rise in volume while price holds above the **$77.2K** True Market Mean would show wider support.

## Altcoins
- Most of the top 500 altcoins outperformed BTC over 30 days.
- The advance stalled this week: **6%** of altcoins at a 30-day high, down from **49% on Sep 22**.
- Leverage is modest: only **19%** of altcoins pay funding above the **0.01%** neutral rate, far below the share at every past altcoin top shown.
- Median altcoin Percent Supply in Profit rose from **3% in mid-August to 23%**, the sharpest rise in over a year. Most holders are still underwater, far from the broad profits of the 2021 and 2024 peaks. Confirmation: the median moving **above 50%** would show the recovery has reached most holders.
- Altcoin perpetual Open Interest rose over 30 days in dollars but fell in coin terms: gains come from price, not new positions. Altcoin spot volume has recovered since August but is far below 2024 and 2025 peaks.
- While positions do not build in coin terms, the risk of a forced unwind stays low.

## Conclusion
- Bitcoin is in an early uptrend without broad participation: on-chain support holds, ETF demand has cooled, spot volume is low.
- The ask wall at **$85K-$85.5K** is the near-term test.
- **Confirmation (broadening uptrend):** break above the wall on rising volume, with ETF inflows returning.
- **Weakening / invalidation:** heavier long-term-holder selling into a flat price, or a **daily close below the True Market Mean at $77.2K**.
- Altcoins joined the rally without new leverage, limiting forced-unwind risk.

*Data as-of Sep 28, 2026 (daily on-chain metrics, ETF flows), Sep 29 (S&P 500, LTH profit) and Sep 30 (hourly price, volume, funding, Binance order book); latest points subject to revision.*
