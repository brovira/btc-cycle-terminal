## B) FRAMEWORKS DE NIVEL/RANGO

- **URPD (UTXO Realized Price Distribution) / volumen on-chain por precio** — el framework de nivel más usado en el lote. "Where a large volume of coins are transacted within a particular price band, it is likely to form a strong support (or resistance) level" [week-14]. Ejemplos numéricos: $45k identificado como soporte por Woo (week-9); fortalecido a $46.6k-$48.6k con 1.2M BTC / 6.5% del supply (week-10); zona >$1T ($53.5k) con 1.98M BTC / 10.6% supply (week-14). Advertencia explícita: "should price fall below, it would become a level where overhead supply may form equally strong resistance" [week-10] — el mismo nivel es soporte por arriba y resistencia por debajo.
- **NUPL (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss)** — usado como termómetro de ciclo, no como nivel de precio directo, pero con umbral 0.5 = "key buy the dip level" en bull markets [week-9], y 0.75 = umbral histórico de techo de ciclo, tras el cual LTH "capture remaining unrealised gains" y suele iniciar tendencias bajistas [week-22].
- **Reserve Risk** — oscilador cíclico; techos históricos en valores >0.02, con umbrales escalonados "Heating Up >0.02 / Boiling Over >0.025" [week-15]. Usado para timing de ciclo completo, no de rango de precio.
- **MVRV / MVRV Z-Score** — usado para "techo" relativo del mercado. Umbral explícito: techos históricos en Z-Score >7.0 pero llegando consistentemente a >11 en ciclos previos [week-15].
- **Peak HODL / fases del ciclo (Reserve Risk + LTH supply)** — framework de 3 fases explícito: Fase A "Max Pain" (bottom de ciclo, solo 40-45% de LTH en profit); Fase B "Peak HODL" (inflexión, mayor % de LTH en profit, coincide con romper ATH previo); Fase C "Cycle Tops" (LTH reactivan supply). Se usa el % reactivado desde Peak HODL como medida de proximidad al techo: 2011=12%, 2013=10%, 2017=17%, 2021 (a esa fecha)=9% [week-12].
- **Realized Price como "cost basis" agregado** — usado para calcular niveles derivados: ejemplo, si NUPL vuelve a 0.5 con Realized Price=$14,511 → suelo estimado = 2×$14,511=$29,022 [week-9]. Fórmula explícita: LTH-SOPR multiple × precio 7d-avg permite inferir cost basis agregado de LTH (ej. $36.2k/3.2=$11.3k) [week-24].
- **Stablecoin Supply Ratio (SSR)** — no da nivel de precio, pero se usa como gauge de "poder de compra" disponible; SSR bajo = más poder comprador latente [week-5, week-15, week-18].
- **HODL Waves / Realized Cap HODL Waves** — usado para calibrar madurez del ciclo comparando el % de supply <6 meses vs. techos históricos (>50% en blow-off tops, vs. 35.9% en el momento analizado) [week-11].

- "When LTH-MVRV reaches the red zone (above 20), this generally indicates a global top." [week-50-2020]
- "When SOPR declines during a bull market, this usually corresponds with sideways price movement; then, when it approaches or dips below 1, this consistently results in an upwards price correction." [week-50-2020]
- "A reset in SOPR usually precedes price stabilization and paves the way for the next bull run." [week-3-2021]
- "**NVT Uptrends** are more bearish... **NVT Downtrends** are more bullish... **NVT Trading Sideways** are supportive of the prevailing trend" [week-18-2021]
- Reserve Risk / MVRV-Z / NUPL — tabla de umbrales de techo dada literalmente: Reserve Risk "Heating Up >0.02, Boiling Over >0.025"; MVRV-Z "Heating up >7.0, [extremo] >10.0"; NUPL "Heating Up >0.70, Overheated >0.75" [week-15-2021]
- Binary CDD: "**Trends higher** when LTHs are **distributing**... **Trends lower** when LTHs are **HODLing**" [week-13-2021]; y ciclo de 4 fases (jun-oct sideways=acumulación, oct-dic trending higher=distribución, ene-mar trending lower=retorno de confianza, mar-may sideways=re-acumulación) [week-19-2021]
- Liveliness: "**Downtrend (green):** HODLing domina. **Steep Uptrend (red):** Old Hands spending heavily. **Gentle Uptrend (orange):** spending pero no exit. **Sideways (blue):** acumulación balanceada" [week-13-2021]
- "Should the key on-chain support level between $45k and $47k falls, many of our observations may swap from strong support to heavy resistance." [week-10-2021]
- Spent Output Age Bands como señal binaria a futuro: "should the market experience a strong rally or further capitulation... If [old coins] come roaring back to life... it may indicate that old hand investors are exiting into liquidity or panic selling (bearish). If not, and their coins stay dormant during volatility, it would be a strong indication that conviction to hold remains in play (bullish)." [week-25-2021]
- "Empty mempools" con doble lectura explícita: "1) Demand for on-chain settlement is extremely low (generally bearish) 2) However, this also indicates that long-term investors are not bailing out at these prices (neutral to bullish)" [week-25-2021]
