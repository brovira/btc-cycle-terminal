# The Week On-Chain — The Ceiling Everyone Can See

**Autor:** Glassnode Research · **Fecha:** 2026-09-09

## Executive Summary
- Bitcoin gained **23%** over 21 sessions while equities stood still, yet remains down **10%** on the year (vs. S&P 500 **+13%**).
- Core inflation dropped to a two-year low of **2.5%**, while inflation expectations sit at **3.6%** — the widest gap in three years.
- Three independent signals — LTH cost basis, the liquidation map and the ETF break-even — draw the same ceiling at **$83K–$86K**; spot stopped **1.5%** short of it.
- Selling pressure into the range high is running at less than half of August's pace, and long-term holders have largely stepped back from distribution.
- The strongest confluence of bottom signals this cycle has cleared, and altcoins have not yet rotated capital away from Bitcoin the way they did before prior cycle tops.

## Macro — Outperformance That Still Trails the Year
Over the last 21 sessions Bitcoin returned **23%** while the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and Euro Stoxx 50 were roughly flat to lower — the best relative performance among the seven tracked assets on that window. Year-to-date the ranking flips: Bitcoin is still down **10%** since January, versus **+13%** for the S&P 500, with oil the year's top performer. The recent rally has only recovered a fraction of the ground lost earlier in the year.

## Macro — Rates Stay Restrictive, Inflation Data Disagrees
The US 10-Year Treasury yield closed at **4.8%**, matching its two-year high, and the 2-Year yield trades roughly **63 basis points** above the **3.75%** federal funds target — a bond market still leaning toward tighter policy. That stance is not confirmed by inflation prints: US core inflation has fallen to a two-year low of **2.5%**, while inflation expectations remain at **3.6%**, the widest expectations/data gap in three years. Two catalysts sit directly ahead: the August CPI print (September 11, 2026) and the FOMC decision (September 16, 2026). A core print moving back toward expectations would support the tighter-policy case; a print holding near its lows would suggest yields have outrun the data.

## On-chain — Three Independent Readings Draw the Same Ceiling
Spot tested the overhead zone flagged the prior week without breaking it: a higher high was printed on September 3, 2026, stopping **1.5%** below the band, before settling into a range just under **$80K**.

The **Long-Term Holder Cost Basis Distribution** shows why the zone holds: roughly **1.07M BTC** was accumulated between **$83K and $86K**, almost entirely by long-term holders, with the single heaviest cluster near **$85K** and essentially unchanged over 30 days. Beneath it, the market has rebuilt a floor directly under spot — supply acquired between **$76K and $82K** (mostly recent buyers) has grown, while the older **$62K–$65K** accumulation floor has thinned as those coins rotated out.

The **BTC Futures Liquidation Heatmap** draws an equivalent ceiling: the short-liquidation shelf between **$82K and $86K** has grown **21%** since the August 19, 2026 squeeze, even as the overall map shrank by roughly a third, leaving that shelf near the largest share of modelled liquidation mass on record. Below spot, a long-liquidation cluster between **$60K and $63K** remains intact. A sustained break above **$86K** would consume the densest short-side fuel on the map; a loss of **$63K** would begin working through the long side.

A third, independent source — the **US spot ETF complex** — reaches the same level from a valuation angle. Measured on coins created since launch, the complex breaks even near **$86K** and has closed below that mark for **228 consecutive sessions**. Its paper loss bottomed near **$18B** on February 5, 2026 and has since narrowed to roughly **$3.9B**, the closest the complex has come to break-even since January. Corporate treasuries break even lower, near **$80.5K**, just under spot. In total, five tracked cost-basis models sit above current price, ranging from the True Market Mean at **$76.6K** to the ETF break-even at **$86K** — a cluster of real cost bases forming the resistance overhead.

## On-chain — Sellers Have Not Shown Up
The **Sell-Side Risk Ratio** (realized profit plus realized loss over Realized Cap, 7-day) has fallen to **7 basis points per day**, less than half the **16 bps** printed at the August peak, and well below the **35 bps** and **23 bps** spikes seen at the July 2025 and October 2025 highs respectively. Long-term holders' share of realized profit has dropped to **47%** from **88%** at the August peak, and the realized-profit spike on September 3, 2026 was under half the size of August's. Recent buyers, not long-term holders, are doing the limited selling that is occurring. A sustained move back above **16 bps/day** would signal the return of August-sized selling; absent that, the market is described as short of sellers at current prices.

## On-chain — Bottom-Signal Confluence Has Cleared
Across the 45 indicators on Glassnode's Market Compass board, the share reading in the coldest band peaked at **82%** in the week of June 29, 2026 and stayed above its long-run median for **41 consecutive weeks** — the strongest bottom-signal confluence of the cycle. That reading has since dropped to **2%** in the latest full week as valuations repaired through the rally. The board has not swung to euphoria, however: three-quarters of indicators still sit below the midpoint of their own history, and **43 weeks** have passed without a majority reading above 50. A majority crossing that midpoint would be the clearest confirmation of a cycle-position turn.

## Off-chain — No Rotation Into Altcoins Yet
Altcoin market cap has risen **21%** over the past month, but the 90-day change in altcoin market-cap share reads **-0.9 points**, meaning altcoins have gained in dollar terms without gaining ground on Bitcoin. Historically, three of the four major Bitcoin price peaks charted saw the altcoin share of combined Bitcoin/altcoin market cap surge at least **2.8 points** over the preceding 90 days (the December 2017 peak is the exception). That precedent-based rotation signal — a 90-day altcoin-share gain of **2.8 points or more** with Bitcoin near its all-time high — is not currently present.

## Conclusion
Bitcoin is consolidating just beneath a ceiling confirmed by three independent sources — long-term holder cost basis, the futures liquidation map and the ETF break-even — all converging on **$83K–$86K**. The setup is a range with a repaired floor and an untested top, distinguished from August mainly by the absence of sellers: sell-side pressure running at less than half of August's pace and long-term holders stepping back from distribution, even as derivatives-side resistance overhead thickened.

**Escenario alcista / confirmación:** a sustained close above **$86K** while the Sell-Side Risk Ratio remains subdued would confirm absorption of the ceiling.

**Escenario de invalidación:** a return of the Sell-Side Risk Ratio above **16 basis points/day**, or a loss of the **$62K–$65K** support floor, would invalidate the current constructive read.
