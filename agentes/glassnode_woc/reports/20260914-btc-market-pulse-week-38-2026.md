## Executive Summary
- Bitcoin sits near **$76,800**, down **4.4%** on the week, but has held a **$76,600–$80,400** range, with every close since Thursday defending the **$76,600** floor.
- Spot conditions have softened: momentum is fading and spot flows on exchanges have turned net-sell, though volume stays unremarkable.
- Leverage in derivatives is high but no longer expanding; taker flow in perpetuals has turned sharply negative.
- US spot ETF demand reversed from strong inflows to outflows this week, alongside a drop in ETF trading volume.
- On-chain usage cooled but stays within normal ranges; capital inflows into the realized cap and supply profitability remain elevated, signaling the pullback has not (yet) broken the constructive backdrop.

## Spot Market — Momentum Fades, Selling Emerges
Price momentum dropped from **54.6** to **46.5**, still inside its statistical bands. Spot cumulative volume delta (CVD) fell from **-$29.7M** to **-$142.7M**, breaching its low band of **-$115.3M** — a signal of net selling flowing through centralized exchanges. Spot volume itself declined modestly, from **$5.3B** to **$4.9B**, remaining within its normal range, so the selling pressure is not (yet) accompanied by a volume spike.

## Derivatives — Leverage Plateaus, Taker Flow Turns Negative
Futures open interest edged down from **$37.1B** to **$36.4B** but still sits above its high band of **$36.0B**, so leverage remains stretched even as it stops growing. Long-side funding payments ticked up slightly, from **$1.3M** to **$1.4M**, staying inside normal bands. Perpetual CVD is the standout: it collapsed from **-$85.6M** to **-$605.9M**, far below its low band of **-$233.9M**, pointing to aggressive taker-driven selling in perps.

In options, open interest holds near **$40.0B**, above its high band. The volatility spread widened to **-22.34%**, below its low band, while the 25-delta skew rose from **-2.05%** to **0.94%** yet remains below its low band of **3.75%** — options positioning is still tilted defensively even as it improves off the week's lows.

## ETF Flows & On-Chain Activity — Demand Reverses, Usage Cools
US spot ETF flows flipped from **$753.2M** of net inflows to **$467.5M** of net outflows, while weekly ETF trade volume dropped from **$12.1B** to **$8.7B** — both readings below their low bands, marking a clear reversal in institutional demand this week. ETF MVRV eased to **1.34**, staying within its normal bands.

On-chain network activity cooled but stayed inside its normal range: fee volume fell to **$202.6K**, transfer volume to **$4.0B**, and active addresses to **610.8K**.

## Capital Flows & Profitability — Still Constructive Despite the Pullback
Capital continues to flow into the network: the monthly realized cap change rose to **1.0%**, above its upper band of **0.2%**. The STH-to-LTH supply ratio (**13.7%**) and hot capital share (**17.6%**) both sit within normal bands, showing no unusual rotation between short- and long-term holders.

Profitability remains elevated but is easing off its highs: **66.2%** of circulating supply is in profit and net unrealized profit/loss (NUPL) reads **8.4%**, both above their high bands. The realized profit/loss ratio fell from **1.0** to **0.5** — still positive and close to its high band, meaning realized gains still outweigh realized losses, just by a narrower margin than last week.

## Key Levels & Read-Through
- **Support:** the **$76,600** level has held on every close since Thursday.
- **Range:** price has stayed contained within **$76,600–$80,400** through the pullback.
- Spot, derivatives, and ETF flows all lean to the sell side this week, and taker selling in perps is aggressive. Yet leverage is not unwinding, loss-taking stays contained, and the realized-cap inflow plus high profitable-supply share suggest the market is absorbing the selling pressure rather than breaking down. A close below **$76,600**, or a swing in perpetual CVD/ETF flows back toward their low bands persisting into next week, would be the signal that the sell-side pressure is starting to dominate rather than being absorbed.
