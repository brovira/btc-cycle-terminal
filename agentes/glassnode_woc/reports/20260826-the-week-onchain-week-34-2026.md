## Executive Summary
- A record single-day short liquidation event on **August 19, 2026** — the largest since 2019 — ignited a rally that burned through **86%** of the liquidation clusters in its path.
- The move was funded, not just triggered: **$2.23B** in ETF creations over the squeeze window, falling exchange balances, and simultaneous buying across every wallet-size cohort.
- The cycle composite (median of 45 indicators) climbed out of its cold/cool bands to the neutral boundary at **40**, after seven months below it.
- A wall of overhead supply has converged into a tight band, roughly **$81K-86K**, combining cost-basis shelves, a re-laddered order book, the dealer gamma flip and the surviving liquidation cluster.
- The options market prices a hold into late September: **$83.3K** is the confirmation level above; the **$70.0K** STH cost basis and the **$62K-65K** floor are the tests below.

## Derivatives — A Record Short Flush
On **August 19, 2026**, Glassnode's feed recorded the largest single day of short liquidations since 2019 (the feed excludes Hyperliquid, so real totals were likely higher). Across the whole squeeze window, **85%** of all liquidated volume was short-side. The rally then consumed the liquidation clusters sitting in its own price path, cutting modelled fuel there by **86%**. What is left: a dense shelf of short-liquidation levels at **$82K-86K** just overhead, and a smaller pocket of long-liquidation fuel left behind at **$60.5K-62.4K**.

## Derivatives — Open Interest and Funding Stay Calm
Futures open interest measured in coin terms *shrank* **11%** across the squeeze window — the dollar value of OI rose only because price rose, meaning liquidated shorts were not replaced with fresh contracts. Funding stayed near its neutral baseline throughout, with occasional negative prints afterward. Glassnode frames this as a stop-cluster flush rather than a leverage-chased rally: no crowded short base beforehand, no long chase after. A rebuilding coin-margined OI alongside rising funding would be the first sign leverage is returning to the move.

## Flows — ETFs Fund the Continuation
US spot ETFs took in **$2.23B** over the squeeze window with zero outflow days — the strongest seven-day intake of the year. The single best day was the largest creation since **January 14, 2026**, and price reclaimed the Short-Term Holder Cost Basis along the way. Daily on-chain turnover during the squeeze week averaged **$2.4B** — active, but roughly half the January-February 2026 pace and a third below the same period last August, so the rally ran without record-breaking transactional activity.

## On-Chain — Supply Rotates Toward Institutional Custody
Since the **June 30, 2026** low, wallets holding **1k-10k BTC** have distributed **50.5K BTC**, while the largest cohort (**>100k BTC** — exchanges, custodians, ETF wrappers) absorbed **59.1K BTC** over the same stretch. Within the squeeze week alone, the custody band gained **31.5K BTC**, a scale that matches (though isn't traced coin-for-coin to) the week's ETF creations — consistent with coins moving from pressured whale wallets onto institutional rails.

## On-Chain — Broad Accumulation Breadth
The 30-day Accumulation Trend Score has sat at or above the neutral **0.5** line across all six wallet-size cohorts for **20 consecutive days** starting **August 5, 2026** — the most persistent all-cohort accumulation stretch since a 22-day run in late 2024. The squeeze days themselves were led by larger wallets, while smaller cohorts built conviction over the full month. Any cohort dropping back below 0.5 would be the first crack in that breadth.

## Cycle — Composite Exits the Cold Zone
The cycle composite (median of 45 cycle indicators) rose back to its neutral boundary of **40** after seven months in the cold/cool bands. Prior comparable stays occurred in **2014-2015, 2018-2019, 2020 and 2022-2023**; each sat in the low zone of its respective cycle. The gauge doesn't predict the speed of recovery, but places the current reading early on the cycle map, far from prior top conditions.

## Cross-Asset — Narrow, Large-Cap-Led Advance
Over the past month, the large-cap crypto cohort returned **20.6%**, matching Bitcoin, while the small-cap cohort managed only **6.0%** — a profile typical of early recoveries where liquidity concentrates in the most liquid names first. Across the squeeze window, Bitcoin gained **25%** while the S&P 500 slipped **1.7%**, and the rolling one-month BTC/equities correlation collapsed toward zero (the inverse relationship with the dollar held). Fast-window decorrelations like this mean-reverted twice already in 2025, so it isn't yet structural, but a rally running against equity weakness can't be explained by equity-beta alone.

## On-Chain — The Cost-Basis Map Brackets Price
Below spot, the **$62K-65K** band holds the buyers of the June-August base, roughly two-thirds of it short-term holder supply defending recent entries. Above spot, the first heavy structure sits at **$83K-86K**, almost entirely long-term holder supply that held through the whole drawdown. The Short-Term Holder Cost Basis, now back underfoot, sits at **$70.0K**. A push into $83K-86K would test whether those long-term holders are willing to sell at break-even.

## Market Structure — Order Book Rebuilds Its Offer Side
As price rose, resting asks in the 100-1000bps band above the mid grew **41%** over the window's final five days, while bids across the whole visible book thinned **32%** over its last seven days — sellers positioning above rather than buyers pulling bids from below. Combined with the cost-basis shelf, the dealer gamma flip, and the surviving liquidation shelf, every overhead structure Glassnode tracks now converges into a single band: the self-custody cost-basis shelf begins at **$80.8K**, dealer gamma flips negative at **$82.3K**, the surviving liquidation shelf runs to **$86K**, and the patient-supply wall fills **$83K-86K** — an aggregate resistance zone of roughly **$81K-86K**.

## Off-Chain — Options Price a Hold
The two heaviest upcoming expiries — this Friday's **$10.4B** and the September quarterly's **$13.7B** — both carry max pain well below spot: **$69K** and **$70K** respectively against a spot price of **$79K**. By the **September 25, 2026** expiry, the middle 70% of strike-implied outcomes spans **$69.0K-89.7K**, with the median close to spot — the market is pricing a hold inside the walls, not a breakout or a retest of the floor.

## Conclusion — Scenarios and Invalidation
Glassnode frames this as a **staged recovery**: the largest short flush on record started the move, ETF creations and exchange outflows funded its continuation, and the cycle composite still reads early-cycle. The scenarios laid out:
- **Confirmation (bullish):** settled closes beyond **$83.3K** with ETF intake holding would show the overhead wall being absorbed rather than respected.
- **First warning sign:** weakness showing up first at the **$70.0K** Short-Term Holder Cost Basis.
- **Deeper test:** a break of the **$62K-65K** floor held by the same recent buyers who funded the squeeze.
- **Full invalidation:** a return to the **$62.9K** squeeze origin would unwind the entire episode.
