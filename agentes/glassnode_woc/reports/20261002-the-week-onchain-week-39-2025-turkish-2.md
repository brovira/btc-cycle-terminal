# The Week On-Chain — Strength Meets a Wall

## Executive Summary
- US spot ETFs took in roughly **$1B on each of two days** in late September; inflows have shrunk since and price has moved sideways.
- Profit-taking is light versus past peaks, but long-term holders stepped up their selling this week.
- Over the last 30 sessions Bitcoin mostly beat the S&P 500, yet this week it fell harder than equities.
- A sell-order wall at **$85K-$85.5K** caps price. The **True Market Mean at $77,200** is the first support below.
- Total volume sits near the bottom of its post-ETF-launch range, so the advance remains early and speculative.
- Altcoins outperformed Bitcoin over the past month with little froth or new leverage, but their rally stalled this week.

## ETF Demand Cools After a Spike
- Net inflows peaked on **Sep 21 and Sep 22 (~$1B each)**; the first of those days was nearly the largest in a year.
- Inflows have shrunk every trading day since, down to **$24M on Sep 28** (latest data point), while price went flat.
- Last week's report flagged accelerating ETF buying. Funds are still buying, but at a small fraction of that pace.
- A return to near-peak inflows would be the clearest sign of renewed ETF demand.

## Profit-Taking: Light Overall, but Sellers Have Changed
- Weekly **Net Realized Profit/Loss** is well below the average week at the 2024 and 2025 peaks; the current pace resembles the start of the late-2023 uptrend.
- Holders of coins **>155 days** nearly **doubled** their realized profit in the week to Sep 29 versus the breakout week. Their share of all realized profit rose from **34% to 55%**.
- If weekly profit-taking climbs toward past-peak levels, it would show these holders are selling into strength.

## Bitcoin vs. the S&P 500
- Over the last 30 sessions, Bitcoin reacted less to S&P down days than up days (the green-shaded pattern held since **Aug 20**), but the window still contains the August rally.
- That edge vanished this week: **Sep 22 to Sep 29, BTC -2.9% vs. S&P 500 -1.2%**, and BTC fell harder than the index on its down days.
- If August sessions roll out of the window and the green period turns red, the edge against equities disappears.
- Share of the last 30 sessions in which BTC beat the S&P 500 moved **above 50% on Sep 24**, for the first time since mid-May. The margin is thin; a few losing sessions could push it back under half.
- Entering October with the share **above 50%** would indicate the edge is more than a short streak.

## Key Price Levels
- Last week's report named **$84K** as the level to hold. Price has since slipped just below it, and a sell-order wall on the Binance spot book now caps it from above.
- **Wall: $85K-$85.5K**. It appeared on Sep 24 and has since tripled in size. Price has pushed into its lower half but never through it.
- While the orders remain, the wall is the most important level. A sustained break **above $85.5K** would clear the largest resistance on the book.
- **Short-Term Holder Cost Basis (coins held <155 days): $73,300**, with price about **13% above** it, so recent buyers are in profit. In the 2023 and 2024 uptrends price stayed above this line for months. A drop below it would put recent buyers back at a loss, as for most of H1 2026.
- **True Market Mean (estimate of the average price paid by active investors): $77,200**, about **8% below** price. Price has held above it since **Sep 18**.
- Periods above both the True Market Mean and the STH Cost Basis marked the stronger phases of the 2023-2025 uptrend.
- **First support to watch: $77,200.** A **daily close below $77,200** would end the current regime.
- (Inferred, not stated: the percentages imply spot around $83K-$84K.)

## Volume: Early and Speculative
- Last week's report noted spot volume had more than doubled from the August low; that recovery started from a very low base.
- Combined spot-exchange plus US spot ETF volume averages about **$6.4B per day**, near the bottom of its range since ETF launch.
- With so little volume behind it, the advance is early and speculative; broad demand has not arrived.
- A sustained volume increase while price holds above the True Market Mean would signal the uptrend is gaining wider support.

## Altcoins: Outperformance Without Froth, Now Stalling
- Most of the top 500 rose more than Bitcoin over 30 days. The rally stalled this week: **6%** of altcoins are at a 30-day high, down from **49% on Sep 22**.
- Leverage stayed modest. Funding above the **0.01% neutral rate** signals crowded longs; only **19%** of altcoins pay above it, far below the share at every past altcoin peak on the chart.
- Median altcoin **Percent Supply in Profit** rose from **3% (mid-August) to 23%**, the sharpest rise in over a year. Most holders are still underwater, far from the broad profits of the 2021 and 2024 peaks. A median **above 50%** would show the recovery has reached most holders.
- Altcoin perpetual **Open Interest** rose in dollar terms over 30 days but fell in coin terms: gains come from higher prices, not new positions. Altcoin spot volume has recovered since August but remains well below 2024 and 2025 peaks.
- Unless positions build up in coin terms, the risk of a forced unwind stays low.

## Conclusion
Bitcoin is in an early uptrend that has not yet drawn broad participation: on-chain support holds, but ETF demand has cooled and spot volume is low. The sell wall above price is the near-term test.

**Confirmation (uptrend broadening):**
- A breakout above the wall with rising volume.
- ETF inflows returning toward the late-September pace.

**Weakening / invalidation:**
- Long-term holders selling more heavily into flat price.
- A **daily close below $77,200 (True Market Mean)**.

Altcoins joined the rally without fresh leverage, which limits forced-unwind risk.
